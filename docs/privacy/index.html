Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data RestockRally collects. Last updated October 27, 2026.

Google Analytics 4

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4, a service from Google LLC, to measure visits and improve the site. Google Analytics 4 loads only after you click Accept in our cookie banner. It sets cookies such as _ga and collects data like pages viewed, device type and approximate location. We turn off Google signals and ad personalization. You can change your choice any time with the Cookie choices button in the footer. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy focused tool that does not use cookies, to count page views.

Contact form

Messages are delivered by FormSubmit to our inbox and used only to reply.

Advertising and affiliate links

If we add advertising (such as Google AdSense) or affiliate links, partners may use cookies subject to your consent where required. We will update this policy first.

Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete data you sent us through the contact form. We do not sell personal information.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13.
