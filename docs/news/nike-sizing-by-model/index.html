Nike sizing by model: charts, conversions and how to measure

By the RestockRally desk

Nike publishes one men's and one women's footwear size chart, not a separate chart for every model. Here is how to measure, convert men's and women's sizes, and read the CM figure on a box.

Last updated November 11, 2026 at 1:50 PM ET

Nike does not publish a separate size chart for every sneaker model. It publishes men's and women's footwear size charts, plus a kids' chart, and a guide on how to measure your foot at home. Those charts are the starting point for any model. For a specific shoe, also read any fit notes on that product page and, if you can, try the pair on.

Measure first, then use the chart

Nike's measurement guide, last updated 21 October 2026, says to tape paper to a hard floor (not carpet), stand with your heel against a wall, trace each foot, and measure from the back of the heel to the tip of your longest toe in centimeters. Measure both feet and use the larger number. If you fall between two sizes, Nike's size charts say to go up.

The same guide says to measure at the end of the day, when feet have swollen, and to wear the socks you will wear with the shoes. A thick running sock, it notes, can change the fit by half a size. Leave about a thumb's width of space between your longest toe and the end of the shoe.

Men's, women's and unisex listings

Nike says women's sizes run 1.5 sizes larger than men's for the same foot length. To convert a women's size to a men's size, subtract 1.5. To convert a men's size to a women's size, add 1.5. On Nike's charts, that means a women's 8 is a men's 6.5, a women's 9 is a men's 7.5, and a women's 10 is a men's 8.5.

Some Nike styles are unisex and listed only in men's sizes. If you usually wear a women's 8, Nike says to order a 6.5 in those styles. Width is not the same across men's and women's shoes, so a converted size gets the length right but not automatically the fit. If a converted pair feels narrow, Nike says to check whether the style comes in a wide.

What changes from one model to another

The official charts give length, not a promise that every model will feel the same. Nike's measurement guide also notes that a size 10 is not a fixed length from brand to brand, and that it can vary by style within a brand. That is why this page does not invent a "size down in Dunks" or "size up in Air Max" rule. Those claims are not on Nike's size charts. Use the chart for length, then confirm the specific model on its product page or in a store.

Nike does call out width options. The men's and women's charts say wide shoes have about 1/4 inch (6.35 mm) more girth in the toe box than regular width, and extra wide shoes have about 1/2 inch (1.27 cm) more. The measurement guide says Nike offers wide and extra wide versions of many styles, including the Pegasus and the Monarch, and that a wide version of the same size gives room across the ball without adding length.

Kids' C and Y sizes, and the CM on the box

Kids' listings use C and Y sizes. We explain that split, and how it maps to adult sizes, in Nike kids' shoe sizes explained. Nike's kids' footwear size chart is the official table.

Both the men's and women's charts carry this warning: the CM size displayed on shoe boxes and labels is different than Foot Length (cm). Do not treat the CM figure on a box as your measured foot length. Match your measurement to the Foot Length row on the chart, then read across for the US, UK, EU and CM/JP figures Nike prints.

If the pair does not fit

For a purchase from Nike, the place to check return rules is Nike's return policy. Launch purchases can have tighter terms; see our guide to SNKRS returns and Nike's terms of sale. A purchase from another store follows that store's policy, not Nike's.
