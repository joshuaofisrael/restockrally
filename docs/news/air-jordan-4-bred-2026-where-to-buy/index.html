Air Jordan 4 "Bred" 2026: where to buy on November 27

By the RestockRally desk

The Air Jordan 4 Retro OG "Bred" (IX8180-001) is listed on Nike SNKRS for November 27, 2026 at $230, with family sizes. The stores named so far, and how to buy.

Last updated November 12, 2026 at 11:30 AM ET

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS page lists the Air Jordan 4 Retro OG "Bred" (IX8180-001) for November 27, 2026 at $230, with big kids' sizes at $165 on the same page. That is the Friday after Thanksgiving.

Release details

Nike describes the pair as the AJ4's original 1989 launch colorway, "remastered to the original specs", with a black nubuck upper, Fire Red and Cement Grey accents, Nike Air heel branding and a Jordan Flight tongue label. Sole Retriever lists the colorway as Black/Cement Grey-Summit White-Fire Red and family sizing at $230 men's, $165 grade school (IX4857-001), $105 preschool (IX4879-001) and $90 toddler (IX4880-001).

Shops to check

Sneaker News lists Nike SNKRS, Nikestore, Foot Locker, Champs, DICK'S, SNIPES US, Sneaker Politics and TAKOUT NY, all marked as standard releases and "Coming Soon" when we read it on 12 November. Sneaker Files adds Finish Line, JD Sports, Hibbett and DSG. Sneaker Files also says in one paragraph that the date moved to November 28, while its quick facts and Nike's page both say November 27. We go with Nike's date; check the SNKRS page for your local launch time.

How to buy

  • Set your size in the SNKRS app first. Our Nike sizing guide and kids' size guide cover Jordan sizing.
  • Read how SNKRS Draw and Line launches work and SNKRS vs retailers before the morning.
  • Enter raffles only on a store's own site or app, and never pay to enter. See how to spot a fake release ad or raffle.
  • If you miss out and look at resale, read how StockX and GOAT verify pairs.

The pair is on our release calendar and the Jordan hub. Confirm the price and time on the retailer's own page before release day.
