October restocks: Nike Mind, Air Bakin and Air Max Phenomena came back

By the RestockRally desk

Nike Mind 001 and 002, the Air Bakin High SP and the Air Max Phenomena all restocked in October 2026. Here is what came back and where.

Last updated October 27, 2026

The short answer: three notable Nike pairs restocked in October: the Nike Mind 001 and 002 on October 15, the Air Bakin High SP "Black/Varsity Red" at Foot Locker on October 22, and the Air Max Phenomena earlier in the month.

Nike Mind 001 and 002

Sole Retriever reported that Nike scheduled a Mind restock for October 15 through the Nike App and Nike.com, not SNKRS, using its Let Everyone Order method. The report listed the Mind 001 in Black, Solar Red and Work Blue at $95 and the Mind 002 Work Blue at $145.

Air Bakin High SP "Black/Varsity Red"

According to CartTactic, the Air Bakin High SP (style code IQ5365‑600) restocked online at Foot Locker on the night of October 22 for $170 in men's sizes.

Air Max Phenomena

NJ.com reported on October 7 that Nike restocked the Air Max Phenomena sneaker loafer in black and a new cacao wow color, at $155.

Restocks sell through fast and stock changes by the minute. Our restocks page keeps a sourced log, and every entry links to the original report.
