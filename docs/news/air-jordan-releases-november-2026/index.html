Every Air Jordan releasing in November 2026

By the RestockRally desk

Space Jam Air Jordan 11s, the Air Jordan 6 White Infrared and the Air Jordan 4 Bred lead Jordan Brand's November 2026 lineup.

Last updated October 28, 2026

The short answer: November is a heavy month for Jordan Brand, with a Space Jam collection for the film's 30th anniversary, the Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" on November 7 and the Air Jordan 4 "Bred" on November 27, according to Footwear News and the SoleInsider November calendar.

Early November

Footwear News lists the Air Jordan 41 for November 5 at $205 as part of the Space Jam collection. SoleInsider shows an Air Jordan 11 MCS cleat in a Space Jam theme the same day at $225. On November 7 the Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" (CT8529‑105) arrives at $215, alongside the Air Jordan 15 and Air Jordan 17 Low "Black Pack" at $245, per Footwear News.

Mid month

The women's Air Jordan 11 "Magic Ball" and "Lapis" are both listed for November 11 at $235 on SoleInsider. Footwear News says the "Magic Ball" pair gets an extended size run up to the equivalent of a men's 12.5. The Air Jordan 11 "Green Screen" (IQ5700‑900) follows on November 14 at $255.

Late November

SoleInsider lists three Air Jordan 3 pairs for November 21: the "Not Nice" at $215 and the grade school "Lola Bunny" and "Santa" at $155 each. Footwear News still showed the "Not Nice" price as TBD when it published, so check the retailer page. The month closes with the Air Jordan 4 "Bred" on November 27 at $230.

All of these pairs are in our release calendar. Dates and prices can change, so confirm on the retailer's page before release day.
