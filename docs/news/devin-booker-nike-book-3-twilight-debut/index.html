Nike November 16, 2026 at 9:00 AM ET

Devin Booker debuts the Nike Book 3 in an all-black "Twilight" colourway

By the RestockRally desk

The Phoenix Suns guard wore his third Nike signature shoe on court for the first time. Nike has not announced the model, so there is no release date or price yet.

Last updated November 16, 2026 at 9:00 AM ET

Devin Booker has given the first look at his next Nike signature shoe. Sneaker News posted on X on Sunday 15 November that the Phoenix Suns guard debuts the Nike Book 3 in an all-black colourway that Sneaker News calls the “Twilight” edition.

What we know

Sneaker News says Booker wore the shoe on court, and that the all-black finish makes the finer design details hard to make out. It describes the look as continuing the understated style of his line.

What we do not know

Nike has not officially announced the Book 3, so performance details, price and release date are not public, according to Sneaker News. It is also not known whether the "Twilight" colourway will be sold at retail. We will add details when Nike confirms them.

The release pattern

Sneaker News notes that the Book 1 stayed on the market for nearly two years in dozens of colourways, while the Book 2 debuted in late 2025 and reached stores in early 2026. A Book 3 debut about a year later points to a more regular yearly cycle, with Booker showing new models late in the year before a retail launch early the next, the outlet says. That would suggest an early 2027 release, but that is an inference, not a confirmed date.
