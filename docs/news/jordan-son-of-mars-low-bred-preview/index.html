Jordan Son of Mars Low "Bred" preview: black and Fire Red, style JA4152-010

By the RestockRally desk

Sneaker News details an upcoming Jordan Son of Mars Low in a black-and-Fire Red Bred treatment tied to the Air Jordan 4 Bred cycle. Retail is listed at $165; no firm drop day yet.

Last updated November 9, 2026 at 7:00 AM ET

Sneaker News has detailed an upcoming Jordan Son of Mars Low in a black-and-Fire Red treatment associated with the Bred colour family. The outlet positions the shoe alongside the expected return of the Air Jordan 4 Bred, but it does not give a firm day for the Son of Mars Low release. This is a product preview, not a verified report of a November 9 restock or a completed sell-out.

Construction and style code

The report describes black nubuck and patent leather across the upper, with Fire Red accents on the midsole, inner tongue and eyestay. The Son of Mars Low is a hybrid that references elements of the Air Jordan 3, 4, 5 and 6. Sneaker News identifies the pair by style code JA4152-010, which is the detail to use when comparing retailer listings.

Price and availability limits

The outlet lists a $165 retail price in men's sizing and says the pair is expected through Nike and selected retailers. Its buying panel gives 2026 as the release year rather than a specific date. No inventory quantity, launch time or confirmed retailer allocation is established. A promotional link or third-party widget is not a guarantee of stock, and published images do not prove the shoe has reached stores in a particular market.

Why the distinction matters

A coordinated colorway can draw attention around a more prominent retro release, but this source does not provide sales forecasts or evidence of unusual demand for the hybrid. The immediate development is that images, a style code and a reported price are available. A later retailer listing could settle the precise launch day and geographic availability. Until then, the right label is upcoming, not restocked.
