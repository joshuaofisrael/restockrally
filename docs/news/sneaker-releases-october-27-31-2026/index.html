Sneaker releases this week: October 27 to 31, 2026

By the RestockRally desk

The Air Jordan 3 Fireside, Kobe Mamba Eterno pack, Air Jordan 14 Forest Green and more land between October 27 and 31.

Last updated October 27, 2026

The short answer: the last week of October is packed, with Nike, Jordan, adidas, Converse and Vans releases landing every day from Tuesday to Saturday, according to House of Heat's weekly roundup and the SoleInsider October calendar.

Tuesday and Wednesday

SoleInsider lists the Menace x adidas Adistar Control 5 "Heat Signature" for October 27 at $180 and the Nocturnal x Nike SB Air Force 1 Low the same day at $120. On October 28, the same calendar shows the Nike Air Max Ecstasy Plus "Triple Black" at $180 and the women's Nike First Sight Shadow "Cream" at $145.

Friday is the big day

October 30 carries the heaviest load. SoleInsider lists the women's Air Jordan 3 "Fireside" at $205, the Nike Book 2 "Oogie Boogie" at $155, the Nike Caitlin 1 "Jinx" at $150 and the Nike KD 19 "Slim Reaper" at $165. The calendar notes that the Caitlin 1 and KD 19 dates moved from October 23. The Kobe "Mamba Eterno" pack also lands that day, with the Kobe 3 Low Protro listed at $190.

Saturday closes the month

The Air Jordan 14 "Forest Green" ($215) and the Air Jordan 4 "Light Army" ($220) round out October, per SoleInsider. House of Heat describes the Air Jordan 4 "Light Army" as an EU exclusive, so US buyers should check regional availability before release day.

Every pair above is in our filterable release calendar, where you can sort by brand, date and price. Dates and prices can change, so confirm on the retailer's page before you buy.
