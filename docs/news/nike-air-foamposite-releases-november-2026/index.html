Nike Foamposite releases in November 2026: Mummy Duck, college pack and Dr. Doom

By the RestockRally desk

Nike has at least six Foamposite pairs on the November 2026 calendar, from the Mummy Duck on November 3 to the Dr. Doom on November 27.

Last updated October 28, 2026

The short answer: the Foamposite is all over Nike's November calendar, with pairs listed for November 3, 5, 13, 16 and 27 on the SoleInsider November calendar.

Mummy Duck

SoleInsider lists the Air Foamposite Pro "Mummy Duck" (IZ7806‑001) for November 3 at $230, and a second "Mummy Duck" listing (JA5895‑001) for November 16 at $240. Check the style code on the retailer page so you know which version you are buying.

College pack on November 5

Three school themed Air Foamposite Pro pairs, "FSU," "LSU" and "Ohio State," are listed for November 5 at $250 each. SoleInsider notes the FSU date moved from November 1 and the Ohio State date moved up from January 1. The Air Max Foamdome "Eggplant" lands the same day at $265.

Phantom and Dr. Doom

The Air Foamposite One "Phantom" (IZ1476‑001) is listed for November 13 at $240, and the Air Foamposite Pro "Dr. Doom" (HF0794‑003) closes the month on November 27, also at $240.

Every pair is in our release calendar. Dates often move, so confirm with the retailer before release day.
