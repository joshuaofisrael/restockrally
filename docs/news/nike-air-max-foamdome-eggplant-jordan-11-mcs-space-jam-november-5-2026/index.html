Air Max Foamdome "Eggplant" and Jordan 11 MCS "Space Jam" cleat set for November 5

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS pages list the Air Max Foamdome Eggplant at $265 and the Jordan 11 MCS Space Jam baseball cleat at $225 for November 5.

Last updated October 30, 2026 at 11:30 London

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS app lists two November 5 launches: the Air Max Foamdome "Eggplant" (IX7255‑506) at $265 and the Jordan 11 MCS "Space Jam" baseball cleat (JA7792‑400) at $225.

Air Max Foamdome "Eggplant"

Nike describes the Foamdome as a pairing of the Foamposite with the Air Max Goadome. According to the listing, the molded upper comes in Eggplant, a color Nike ties to 1990s basketball, on top of a chunky midsole with lugged traction. Our restocks log lists the Goadome "Black Pony Hair" for a restock on October 29, as reported by Sneaker News.

Jordan 11 MCS "Space Jam"

Nike says this Jordan 11 baseball cleat celebrates 30 years of Space Jam and the silhouette linked to the film. The SNKRS page credits the Space Jam name and characters to Warner Bros. Entertainment. It is a cleat for baseball, not a lifestyle sneaker, so check the product details before you buy.

Time

Both pages we opened on October 30, 2026 read "Available 11/5 at 3:00 PM" with no time zone printed; SNKRS shows the hour in your device's time zone. The SoleInsider November calendar lists both pairs for November 5 at 10 AM ET with the same prices. Both are in our release calendar, and the rest of the week is in our November 1 to 7 list.
