Kobe 8 Protro "Shanghai Fireworks" returns November 11 for $200

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS page lists the Kobe 8 Protro Shanghai Fireworks, style code IO6259-800, for November 11 at $200, with a Big Kids' listing at $125.

Last updated October 31, 2026 at 7:00 AM ET

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS page lists the Kobe 8 Protro "Shanghai Fireworks" in Laser Orange and Safety Orange (style code IO6259‑800) for Wednesday, November 11 at $200. The same page carries a second listing at $125 under style code IV5459‑800.

What Nike says

Nike says the colorway first released in 2013 and describes the upper as covered in artwork that depicts a fireworks display. According to the listing, the Protro build is low and light, uses a Nike React foam insert, and pairs a soft synthetic upper with grippy traction for quick cuts.

Sizes and price

Nike's upcoming launches list shows both a basketball shoe listing and a Big Kids' Kobe 8 listing for November 11, which matches the two prices on the product page. If you need help picking between C, Y and men's sizes, see our kids' sizing guide.

Time and where to buy

The page we opened on October 31, 2026 read "Available 11/11 at 3:00 PM" with no time zone printed. SNKRS shows the hour in your own device's time zone, so check the page on your phone. The pair is on our Kobe 8 Protro release page and in the release calendar. Our SNKRS Draw and Line explainer covers how launches work. Dates and prices can change, so confirm on the retailer's page before you buy.
