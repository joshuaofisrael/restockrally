NikeSKIMS Waffle Racer drops today in three women's colorways at $130

By the RestockRally desk

Three women's NikeSKIMS Waffle Racer colorways are listed for 10 a.m. ET on Tuesday, November 10, at $130 each, with first come sales at one store.

Last updated November 10, 2026 at 8:40 AM ET

Three women's colorways of the NikeSKIMS Waffle Racer are scheduled to release today, Tuesday, November 10, at 10 a.m. Eastern, according to the Just Drops weekly calendar. Each pair is listed at $130.

The three colorways

- Baroque Brown/Velvet Brown - Light Bone/College Grey - Thunder Blue/Blue Eclipse

All three share the same 10 a.m. Eastern slot, which is 9 a.m. Central, 8 a.m. Mountain and 7 a.m. Pacific.

Where to look

Just Drops lists first come, first served sales at one store for the Baroque Brown/Velvet Brown pair and has not published a store or app method for the other two colorways yet. That makes Nike's own channels and the NikeSKIMS collection pages the first places to check at drop time. The today page is updated through the morning, so look there for any late changes to the store list.

How to approach a three colour drop

With three versions landing at the same minute, picking one colorway in advance gives you the best chance of checking out before sizes sell through. These are women's sizes, so shoppers who usually buy men's sizes should convert first.

Also out today

The NikeSKIMS release shares the Tuesday slate with the Nike Air Max 95 Big Bubble "ACG" at $190 and the Nike Mind 002 Flyknit in Black/Anthracite at $145, both also at 10 a.m. Eastern on SNKRS. Wednesday brings the women's Air Jordan 11 "Magic Ball" and the Kobe 8 Protro "Shanghai Fireworks", which Nike later listed on SNKRS at $200.

Before you pay

Release calendars can change at short notice. Confirm the colorway name and price on the retailer's product page and buy only from Nike or a store you recognise.
