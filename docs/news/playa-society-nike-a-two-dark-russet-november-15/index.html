Playa Society x Nike A'Two "Dark Russet" releases 15 November at $155

By the RestockRally desk

Playa Society's first Nike collaboration dresses A'ja Wilson's A'Two in glossy chocolate leather and suede monogram panels. It drops on 15 November through Nike and selected retailers.

Last updated November 14, 2026 at 8:55 AM ET

The Playa Society x Nike A'Two "Dark Russet" (style code IX6704-200) releases on Sunday, 15 November, for $155 in unisex sizing through Nike and selected retailers, according to Athlon. A second pair, the "Ocean Bliss", drops the same day.

The collaboration

Playa Society is the women's basketball apparel brand founded by Esther Wallace, a former college player. Andscape describes the A'Two as the brand's first official sneaker collaboration with WNBA MVP A'ja Wilson of the Las Vegas Aces, and says the caramel and toffee brown uppers give it a vintage luxury feel.

The shoe

Athlon lists a glossy chocolate-brown leather upper, an orange and brown suede monogram print on the toe and heel tab, checkered mesh panels, a shiny brown Swoosh and laces, a silver Playa Society lace jewel on the tongue and a jewelled heel piece. It notes that $155 is $10 more than a regular A'Two.

How to buy

Check the Nike app and SNKRS on release morning, along with the selected retailers carrying the pair. Neither source gave a launch time or confirmed a draw, so look at your retailer's listing for the method.
