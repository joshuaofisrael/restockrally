Women's Air Jordan 11 "Magic Ball" launches November 11 at $235

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS page lists the women's Air Jordan 11 Magic Ball, style code IZ1800-200, for November 11 at $235, with a Space Jam theme.

Last updated October 31, 2026 at 7:00 AM ET

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS page lists the women's Air Jordan 11 "Magic Ball" (style code IZ1800‑200) for Wednesday, November 11 at $235.

The design

Nike ties this pair to the Magic Ball from Space Jam. The listing describes shifting color, Off Noir and flashes of Metallic Silver on the familiar Air Jordan 11 shape. The page credits the Space Jam name and characters to Warner Bros. Entertainment. RestockRally is not affiliated with Nike or Warner Bros.

The sister pair

Our release calendar also lists a women's Air Jordan 11 "Lapis" for the same date at $235, based on the SoleInsider November calendar. Nike's upcoming list we opened on October 31 showed the Magic Ball but not the Lapis yet, so treat that date as a calendar listing until Nike posts its own page. For the full month, see every Air Jordan releasing in November 2026.

Time and sizing

The page read "Available 11/11 at 3:00 PM" when we opened it, with no time zone printed; SNKRS shows the hour in your device's time zone. This is a women's size run, so check the size guide on the product page before launch day. The pair has its own release page with where to buy links.
