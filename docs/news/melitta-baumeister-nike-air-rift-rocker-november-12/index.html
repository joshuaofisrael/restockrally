Melitta Baumeister x Nike women's Air Rift Rocker drops today on SNKRS at $220

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS pages list the women's Air Rift Rocker by Melitta Baumeister in Black and White (IQ8101-001) and Yukon Brown (IR3982-200) for 12 November at $220.

Last updated November 12, 2026 at 7:50 AM ET

New York designer Melitta Baumeister's reworked Nike Air Rift releases today, Thursday, 12 November. Nike's SNKRS launch page lists the women's Air Rift Rocker in Black and White, style IQ8101-001, at $220, and a second SNKRS page covers the Yukon Brown and Light Iron Ore pair, style IR3982-200.

Release time

DamnKicks puts the US drop at 10:00 a.m. ET on SNKRS, first come, first served, in women's sizes. It says the UK and Spain get both colourways on Nike's sites the same morning, about seven hours before the US.

What makes it different

Nike says Baumeister took hold of the Rift 2 and shaped it into a new shoe. Its description lists patinaed leather over a neoprene-like upper, a grosgrain forefoot strap, clay-like moulded Swoosh logos on the toe tip and laces, and a translucent outsole with a custom "MB" traction pattern over footprint graphics. DamnKicks reports that Baumeister told Complex the midsole was sculpted by hand in clay before becoming the new rocker sole. The original Air Rift, with its split toe, dates from 1996.

Highsnobiety called it the most unusual release of the week.

How to buy

Both colourways sell on the SNKRS app in the US. Sizes are women's, so convert from men's sizing before the drop if you need to. Split-toe shoes fit differently from a standard sneaker, so check Nike's sizing notes on the launch page before you check out.
