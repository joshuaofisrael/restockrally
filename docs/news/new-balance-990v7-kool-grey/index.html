New Balance 990v7 Kool Grey launches with a new generation of the 990

By the RestockRally desk

The Made in USA 990v7 Kool Grey is listed for 6 November at $199.99, marking the first new 990 version since the v6 in 2022, according to HYPE COMPANY.

Last updated November 6, 2026 at 8:00 AM ET

New Balance’s Made in USA 990v7 “Kool Grey” is scheduled to launch on Friday, 6 November, according to HYPE COMPANY’s weekly sneaker release report. (HYPE COMPANY)

The report lists the shoe at $199.99, with a release time of 10 a.m. Eastern on newbalance.com and availability at selected retailers. The colorway combines Kool Grey with Calcium and Light Arctic Grey, and carries the style code U990GR7.

HYPE COMPANY describes the 990v7 as the first new version of the 990 since the v6 arrived in 2022. The updated design returns to suede overlays over mesh, adds a larger N logo on each side and uses a softer interpretation of the v6’s FuelCell foam alongside ENCAP cushioning and a new TPU shank.

The launch gives the long running 990 line a new platform while retaining a restrained grey palette associated with the model. The report frames the release as a major sneaker drop for the week, alongside anniversary themed Jordan releases and other new colorways.

The listed price and launch timing come from HYPE COMPANY’s report. Retailer availability and stock can change, so the scheduled release details should not be taken as a guarantee that every size will remain available.
