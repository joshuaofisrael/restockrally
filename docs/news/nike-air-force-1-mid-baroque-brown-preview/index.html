Nike Air Force 1 Mid "Baroque Brown" preview: IQ0189-200, $125

By the RestockRally desk

Sneaker News publishes images of the Nike Air Force 1 Mid in Baroque Brown pebbled leather. Pairs are reportedly appearing overseas; a firm US launch day is not listed.

Last updated November 9, 2026 at 7:30 AM ET

Sneaker News has published images and details of the Nike Air Force 1 Mid in a Baroque Brown colorway, with pairs reportedly beginning to appear overseas. The outlet describes a Holiday 2026 release but does not provide a confirmed US launch date. Treat this as a product preview, not a claim that a nationwide restock is live.

Design and identification

The reported design uses a predominantly brown, pebbled full-grain leather upper with a silver lace dubrae as the main break from the tonal brown. Sneaker News identifies the style as IQ0189-200. The coverage places the colorway in the seasonal AF1 Mid lane that also includes finishes such as Flax. Those are design observations from the published product images, not evidence of demand or limited production.

What the listing does and does not say

Sneaker News displays a $125 retail price and a 2026 release year in its buying information. The report says the shoe should reach Nike.com and other retailers, but it does not establish a specific US release day, inventory count or confirmed stock at every location. Reported overseas appearances also do not set the timing of domestic distribution.

How to follow the release

The clearest next confirmation is a live retailer product page showing the correct style code, sizes, price and shipping region. Until then, treat the date as pending. This colorway is a separate upcoming AF1 Mid story from this week's scheduled drops, and this report does not claim sell-outs or resale moves.
