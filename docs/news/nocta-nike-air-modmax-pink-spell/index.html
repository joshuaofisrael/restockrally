Drake's NOCTA x Nike Air Modmax "Pink Spell": $180, style IQ6711-600, date still firming up

By the RestockRally desk

New images of the NOCTA Air Modmax "Pink Spell" surfaced this week. Sneaker News and Hypebeast say Holiday 2026 at $180, while Sole Retriever lists 20 January 2027.

Last updated November 12, 2026 at 7:30 AM ET

Drake's NOCTA line with Nike has a new model on the way, the Air Modmax. Sneaker News posted on X on 11 November that the shoe is confirmed for a holiday-season launch at $180, and Hypebeast has since published new images of the first colorway, "Pink Spell."

What we know

Hypebeast lists the colorway as Pink Spell/Volt/Amarillo/Cobalt Tint, style code IQ6711-600, at $180 through Nike. It describes a pink gradient upper with three translucent amber pods at the heel, and says the shoe introduces Nike's new Air Modmax cushioning to the NOCTA range.

The release date is not settled

Sources disagree on timing. Hypebeast and Sneaker News both say Holiday 2026. Sole Retriever's release page lists Wednesday, 20 January 2027 at $180, with the colorway written as Pink Spell/Pink Spell-Amarillo-Volt. Nike has not yet put up a SNKRS launch page that we could find, so treat any date as provisional until it appears there.

How to prepare

The price and style code are consistent across these sources, which makes IQ6711-600 the detail to watch for on SNKRS, the NOCTA site and retailer pages. As a collaboration, it may sell through quickly when it does launch. RestockRally will update this page when Nike confirms a date and method.
