Nike Air Zoom Generation "Gloria James" PE Set for Spring 2027 With Matching LeBron Pinks

By the RestockRally desk

Sneaker News reports Nike will release LeBron's Air Zoom Generation "Gloria" PE in Spring 2027 alongside similar pink colorways for the LeBron 24 and Witness 10; Sneaker Files lists 5 February 2027 at $190 (JA6289-100).

Last updated November 5, 2026 at 9:00 AM ET

The short answer: Nike plans to release LeBron James's Air Zoom Generation "Gloria James" player-exclusive colorway to the public in Spring 2027, with Sneaker Files dating the drop to 5 February 2027 at $190 (style code JA6289-100). Sneaker News reported exclusively on X that the pair arrives alongside similar pink colorways for the LeBron 24 and LeBron Witness 10.

What Sneaker News and Sneaker Files say

Sneaker News posted on 4 November 2026: "EXCLUSIVE: Nike will release the Air Zoom Generation \"Gloria\" PE come Spring 2027. The grail-level PE arrives alongside similar pink colorways for the LeBron 24 and LeBron Witness 10." Its full story is at Sneaker News.

Sneaker Files lists a White/Metallic Silver-Real Pink scheme, a 5 February 2027 release date, SNKRS, Foot Locker and Hibbett among expected channels, and a $190 retail price. The site describes the colorway as originally created as a 2003 player exclusive honouring Gloria James and says the 2027 pair is planned as a general release rather than another tightly limited PE.

Design notes from the cited guides

Sneaker Files says white covers the leather upper, mudguard and midsole, with Real Pink on the heel and outsole, and LeBron's signature logo embroidered on the lace lock. Those details describe the product as reported; they are not a hands-on review of production pairs, which were not yet on sale when the calendars published.

The Air Zoom Generation was LeBron's first signature shoe. Bringing back an unreleased or PE-only tribute colorway more than twenty years later is the collecting hook Sneaker News and Sneaker Files both emphasise. Sneaker Files also notes that LeBron's Hummer H2 inspired elements of the original model's design language—an historical note about the silhouette, not a claim about the 2027 pink materials beyond what the guides state.

The matching pink LeBron 24 and Witness 10 colorways mentioned by Sneaker News are companion products in the same exclusive report. Exact prices, style codes and street dates for those two models should be confirmed when Nike or the sneaker press publish dedicated listings; this article does not invent those fields.

How this sits against other November drops

Restock Rally's existing November 5–7 calendars cover near-term launches such as the Air Jordan 6 White Infrared and New Balance 990v7. The Gloria James Air Zoom Generation is a different planning horizon: a Spring 2027 / early-February date rather than a same-week SNKRS scramble. Shoppers comparing "what's next" should keep those timelines separate so a February 2027 PE release is not mistaken for a November 2026 restock.

How to use this for shopping plans

February 2027 is still months away. Release calendars change, and a scheduled SNKRS listing is not a guarantee of size-run depth in every region. Shoppers who care about this colorway should watch official Nike Basketball pages and the linked Sneaker News / Sneaker Files reports for updates rather than treating secondary-market rumours as inventory proof.

Sneaker Files' FAQ frames the 2027 pair as a general release even though the original PE was scarce. That distinction matters for expectations: wider retail participation is reported as the plan, but "general release" still does not mean unlimited stock in every size.
