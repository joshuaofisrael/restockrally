Official images are out: the Air Jordan 11 "Space Jam" returns on 12 December, with the "Jumpman Jam" tongue back for the first time since 2000

By the RestockRally desk

The Air Jordan 11 "Space Jam" (CT8012-900) releases on 12 December in full family sizing, with men's pairs at $235. Nike says the 2026 pair brings back the "23" heel and the "Jumpman Jam" tongue.

Last updated November 20, 2026 at 8:40 AM ET

The biggest holiday Jordan drop now has official pictures. "OFFICIAL IMAGES: Air Jordan 11 Retro 'Space Jam' (2026)," KicksFinder posted on X overnight, listing a 12 December release and style code CT8012-900 for men's pairs.

Release details

According to the KicksFinder post, prices are $235 for men's, $190 for grade school (378038-900), $105 for preschool (378039-900) and $90 for toddler (378040-900). Nike's release story confirms 12 December on Nike.com, the SNKRS app and at select retailers in full family sizing. Sneaker Bar Detroit lists the same prices.

What is different this time

Nike says the 2026 pair "draws directly from" the movie-worn original and brings back two details: the "23" on the heel and the "Jumpman Jam" tongue, which it says returns for the first time since 2000. Sneaker Bar Detroit describes black mesh and patent leather on the upper, a white midsole, an icy outsole and blue Jumpman branding, with a shape close to the 2016 pair.

Why people care

Michael Jordan wore the black and blue 11 during the 1995 playoffs and again in the 1996 film, which gave it its name. Sneaker Bar Detroit recalls that the 2009 release brought long lines and overnight campouts, and that Nike later called the 2016 version its biggest footwear launch at the time. Expect heavy demand again, especially in men's sizes.

How to plan

Holiday Jordan drops usually go through SNKRS draws as well as retailer raffles, so sign up early. Retailer links and times are usually confirmed in the week before release.
