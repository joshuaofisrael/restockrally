How the adidas CONFIRMED app works: raffles, queues and invites

By the RestockRally desk

adidas CONFIRMED releases limited sneakers by raffle, queue or invite. Here is what adidas says about each format, charges, notifications and setting up your profile.

Last updated November 12, 2026 at 8:02 AM ET

CONFIRMED is the adidas app for limited releases and collaborations. adidas describes it on its CONFIRMED page as the place to explore upcoming releases, enter verified draws and read stories about each drop. Recent adidas collaborations we have covered include the BAPE Crazy Energy+ launch and Wales Bonner's Fall/Winter 2026 collection.

Three ways a product drops

adidas says products on CONFIRMED release in one of three ways:

  • Raffle. You enter for a chance to buy.
  • Queue. First come, first served while stock lasts.
  • Invite. Exclusive access to a release by invitation.

adidas's explainer How CONFIRMED drops work calls these release mechanics and names them Draw, Queue and Invite. It says the choice for each product depends on demand, availability, strategy and distribution, and that some products drop on a simple first come, first served basis instead.

How a CONFIRMED raffle works

According to adidas, each raffle is open for a limited time. You enter before it closes, and winners are selected at random. If you win, you can buy the product; if you do not, you are not charged. You can follow a raffle's progress in the app and check results when they are posted. Winners get a notification from the app with details on how to complete the purchase, so adidas tells users to enable notifications and to check the raffle status in the app.

Why adidas asks you to verify your account

The same explainer says the mechanics let adidas validate each user by evaluating payment details, phone numbers, addresses, location data and more, to try to stop people gaming the system. That is why you must become a member and verify your account before you can buy.

Setting up before a drop

adidas's setup advice is practical. Once you download the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play and create an account, add your size, save your payment details and turn on notifications. The explainer adds: keep the newest version of the app, keep your profile up to date with a valid payment method and address, and enable location services.

Your size choice carries through to the entry, so check it against the official chart first. Our adidas shoe size chart guide explains how adidas converts US, UK, EU and JP sizes, which do not match Nike's conversion exactly.

How it compares with other stores

Raffles on CONFIRMED are random, according to adidas. Foot Locker's app, by contrast, uses a weighted selection that counts loyalty and boosts.
