Sneaker releases this week: November 3 to 7, 2026

By the RestockRally desk

The Air Jordan 6 White Infrared, New Balance 990v7, Jordan Space Jam pack and Nike's college Foamposites headline the week of November 3 to 7.

Last updated November 3, 2026

The short answer: the first full week of November builds to a heavy Thursday, Friday and Saturday, led by the Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" on November 7 and the New Balance 990v7 on November 6, according to Sneaker News' weekly roundup, House of Heat's release list and the Just Drops weekly calendar.

Tuesday and Wednesday

Just Drops lists the Nike Air Foamposite Pro "Mummy Duck" for November 3 at $230 and 10 a.m. ET on SNKRS, alongside the Nike Giannis Freak 8 at $120. Sneaker News describes this week's Giannis Freak 8 as an "Origin Story" colorway honoring the end of his mandatory Greek military service, while Just Drops lists a "Camo" name, so check the listing on the day. On November 4, Just Drops shows the women's Nike Air Max Dolce Leather in Black/Anthracite at $170 on SNKRS.

Thursday: Space Jam and the college Foamposites

November 5 is the busiest day on every calendar we checked. Just Drops lists the first wave of Jordan Brand's Space Jam pieces, including the Jordan 11 Retro MCS "Space Jam" at $225 and the Air Jordan 1 Mid SE "Space Jam" at $140. The Nike Air Foamposite Pro college pack ("Ohio State," "LSU," "TCU" and "FSU") lands the same day at $250 each, per Just Drops, with Sneaker News also placing it on the 5th. New Balance adds the Made in USA 993 "Shadow Blue" at $199.99, and Nike brings back the Air Force 1 Low "What The LA" at $125.

Friday: the 990v7 and signature hoops

Sneaker News calls November 6 "the main event," with the New Balance 990v7 starting a new chapter of the 990 line. Just Drops lists the big kids' 990v7 in Kool Grey at $199.99. The same day brings the adidas Anthony Edwards 3 "With Love," the Nike Book 2 "Desert Night" and the PUMA x Stranger Things collection, which both House of Heat and Sneaker News put on the 6th.

Saturday closes the week

The Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" returns on November 7 at $215 for adult sizes, with SNKRS plus raffles and first-come releases at a range of shops, per Just Drops. Our Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" release guide has the details. House of Heat also lists the Air Jordan 15 and 17 "Black Pack" and the BAPE x adidas collection for Saturday.

Release dates and prices can move, and calendars do not always agree, so confirm on the brand's own launch page before release morning. Every pair is in our filterable release calendar.
