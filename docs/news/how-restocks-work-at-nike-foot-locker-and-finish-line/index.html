How sneaker restocks work at Nike, Foot Locker and Finish Line

By the RestockRally desk

Restocks are not one shared inventory pool. Here is what Nike, Foot Locker and Finish Line actually publish about notifications, launch entries, store stock and STATUS Exclusive Access.

Last updated November 11, 2026 at 1:54 PM ET

A restock is a pair going back on sale after it has already sold out. We explain that idea, and why restocks are rarely announced in advance, in What is a sneaker restock, and how do you catch one?. This page is narrower: it covers only what Nike, Foot Locker and Finish Line say about their own sites, apps and stores. Stock at one seller is not proof of stock at another.

Nike and SNKRS

Nike's Exclusive Access page tells Members to keep the SNKRS app installed and push notifications enabled, because invitations are time limited. That advice is about Exclusive Access offers, not a public restock calendar. Nike does not publish a single schedule of when sold out sizes will return. The live source for a given shoe is that shoe's Nike or SNKRS product page, including the listed size run and any launch instructions on the page.

Regular SNKRS Draw and Line launches are also not restocks. A Draw is a random pick after entries close; a Line works like a queue. See how Draw and Line launches work. If you missed a launch, a later restock, if one happens, is a new availability event on the product page, not a continuation of your earlier entry.

Foot Locker

Foot Locker's US help page on how you are notified if you have secured a release is about launch entries, not a promise that a sold out size will email you later. Foot Locker says push notifications are the main way it will tell you if you have been selected. For store pickup, it usually notifies you the afternoon before the launch date, and you must confirm within 60 minutes of being selected or the reservation is forfeited. For ship to me, it usually notifies you the morning of the launch, and you also get an order confirmation email to the address on your account. You can check the status of an entry in the mobile app. A related help page says push notifications are not required but are highly recommended, and repeats the 60 minute confirmation window for store pickup.

Foot Locker's product information page explains store versus online labels. "Available in Stores" means a style is carried in many store locations, but not all, and Foot Locker says it cannot guarantee product availability in every store. After you select a size, you can enter a zip code to find the six closest stores within a 100 mile radius, then contact a store to check availability and pricing. Store prices may differ from the website. Styles marked "Online Only" are sold on the website and not in stores. Not every in store product is sold online, and inventory status is updated often, so the page says to refresh for the latest results.

Those tools help you check current stock. They are not a confirmed restock date. If you enter a Foot Locker launch, follow the app and the help pages above rather than a third party "restock" screenshot. See also how to spot a fake sneaker release ad or raffle.

Finish Line and STATUS Exclusive Access

Finish Line and JD Sports in the US share the STATUS rewards program. Finish Line's STATUS Exclusive Access page describes an invitation for STATUS members to redeem STATUS points for a chance to buy selected sneakers in store or online before the public release. A successful redemption reserves a pair ahead of the release date. In store events require pickup at the participating JD or Finish Line store on release day. Digital preorder events ship to the member's address on release day. Finish Line says offers are generally made within 48 hours before the sneaker release date, that you are allowed only one STATUS account, and that Exclusive Access is never guaranteed for every launch.

If your size is gone when you try to redeem, or you miss the offer, Finish Line says you can try again on the public release date, which it lists on the Finish Line and JD Sports sneaker release calendars. Look for a STATUS badge on those calendars for shoes that may use this path. Finish Line also says once you redeem, you cannot change the size, you can redeem only one pair in one size, and if you cannot make an in store pickup window you forfeit the reserved pair and the points you used.

STATUS Exclusive Access is a pre release reservation for invited members. It is not the same thing as a Nike SNKRS Exclusive Access invitation, and it is not a sold out restock after launch day. For later stock, check Finish Line's own product page and app. The Finish Line app page says downloading the app is how you get notified about drops.

A practical way to watch all three

Treat each seller separately. Save the official product page, turn on that seller's own notifications if you use them, and do not assume an unsuccessful Nike entry or a sold out Foot Locker size tells you anything about Finish Line, or the other way around.
