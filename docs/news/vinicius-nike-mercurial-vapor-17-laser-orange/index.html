Vinícius Júnior's Laser Orange Mercurial Vapor 17 is set for 12 November at $270

By the RestockRally desk

House of Heat said Nike has unveiled a Laser Orange and Fuchsia Flash Mercurial Vapor 17 player edition inspired by Brazilian funk, releasing on 12 November for $270 in the Elite FG version.

Last updated November 11, 2026 at 9:40 AM ET

Nike has unveiled another Vinícius Júnior Mercurial Vapor 17, in Laser Orange, with a 12 November release, House of Heat reported. The Elite FG version is listed at $270, to sell on Nike.com and at select retailers. The SKU is given as IQ8048-800, in Laser Orange and Fuchsia Flash.

House of Heat said the boots draw on the Brazilian funk music that raised the Real Madrid forward in Rio de Janeiro. The Atomknit upper is described as a gradient from Laser Orange into a deeper Fuchsia Flash around the lower heel, with dark, purplish-black Swoosh logos carrying graphics that nod to Brazil. The footballer's logo appears on the collar and insole. "Acredite," the Portuguese word for "Believe," appears on the insole, and the report said the Portuguese word and its English translation also appear on the outsole.

The launch is Thursday, 12 November. House of Heat presented official product images credited to Nike. A scheduled drop can still move, and stock is not guaranteed in every size or region, so Nike's own listing is the place to confirm price and availability on release morning.

This pair is a player-edition football boot, not a lifestyle sneaker, and the $270 figure is for the Elite FG version as stated in the House of Heat report. Other silos or junior sizes, if they exist, were not priced in that article.
