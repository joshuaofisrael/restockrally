Air Jordan 3 "Fireside": where to buy on October 30

By the RestockRally desk

The women's Air Jordan 3 Fireside drops October 30 at 10 AM ET for $205 on SNKRS. Here is how it sells and where.

Last updated October 28, 2026

The short answer: the women's Air Jordan 3 Retro "Fireside" (CK9246‑200) releases Friday, October 30 at 10 AM ET for $205 on the SNKRS app as a Let Everyone Order launch, according to Just Drops.

Release details

Just Drops lists the colorway as Velvet Brown, Light British Tan, Rust Pink and Pearl White, and says Nike moved the US date from October 3 to October 30. It tracks the pair at 29 retailers, with 23 selling first come, first served and 3 running raffles.

Shops to check

Drop Agenda lists Lapstone & Hammer, Sneaker Politics and Extra Butter as stockists, and notes that limited pairs usually sell by raffle or first come, first served. We only list a raffle on our raffles page after we confirm it on the retailer's own site, so enter only through each shop's own website or app and never pay to enter.

The pair is also in our release calendar. Confirm price and timing on the retailer page before release day.
