adidas D.O.N. Issue #8 "Solar Turbo" lands at 10 AM ET for $120, but adidas US quietly went live hours early

By the RestockRally desk

The adidas D.O.N. Issue #8 "Solar Turbo/Black/Gold Metallic" (KI9285) is listed for November 21 at about 10 AM ET for $120, with men's and grade school sizes at adidas US and several retailers.

Last updated November 21, 2026 at 9:00 AM ET

Donovan Mitchell's eighth signature shoe gets a loud new colourway today. "adidas D.O.N. Issue #8 'Solar Turbo/Black/Gold Metallic' live at 7am PT/10am ET," snkr_twitr posted on X on Saturday morning, in a post marked as an ad.

Price, code and time

The Just Drops November calendar lists the adidas D.O.N. Issue 8 under style code KI9285 for November 21, at about 10:00 AM ET, for $120.

Some pairs went early. "Dropped via adidas US," snkr_twitr posted at about 5:57 AM ET, linking men's and grade school sizes, so check adidas before the 10 AM retailer launches.

Where it is listed

The snkr_twitr post lists men's sizes at adidas US and grade school sizes at adidas US, Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs, Finish Line and JD Sports, with men's pairs also linked at Scheels, Hibbett, Finish Line, JD Sports and Dick's Sporting Goods. The post also mentions discount codes at two retailers. Codes like these change quickly, so check the terms at checkout before you rely on them.

Same colour, different shoe

The same "Solar Turbo/Black" colour name also appears on the adidas Crazy Energy, which snkr_twitr listed for the same 10 AM ET slot. If you want a matching pair, check the style code on each product page so you get the model you meant to buy.

Tips for release morning

A $120 general release is unlikely to need a raffle, but popular sizes can still go first. Load the product page a few minutes early, sign in to your account and save your payment details. Retailer times can differ, so confirm on each store's page. For other pairs out this weekend, see our release calendar.
