Air Jordan 3 "Not Nice List" (IF4396-001) releases Saturday 21 November for $215

By the RestockRally desk

Foot Locker lists the Air Jordan 3 Not Nice List for 21 November at $215, ship to me only. Style code IF4396 001.

Last updated November 20, 2026

The Air Jordan 3 "Not Nice List" releases this Saturday, 21 November. House of Heat's release list for the week puts it on Saturday with the grade school Air Jordan 3 "Santa". Foot Locker's launch page lists the "Jordan Retro 3 Not Nice List" as available on 21 November.

What Foot Locker's page shows

We reloaded that Foot Locker page on 20 November. The visible price is $215. Under entry options, the page says ship to me only. That is an entry you submit, not a pair waiting for whoever loads the page first. The page data timestamps the release at 21 November 2026, 15:00 UTC, which is 10 a.m. Eastern. House of Heat's weekly list names the day and does not print a clock time.

Foot Locker's own code on the page is F4396001. The style code Justfreshkicks published, the one that belongs on the box label, is IF4396 001. Retailer calendars often shorten or reshape that code, so if one search misses, try the other. The style code index and the guide to how to look up a Nike style code show where the code sits on a label.

What it looks like

According to Justfreshkicks, the pair uses cracked black leather with tan showing through on the main panels, black elephant print with a red pattern on the toe and heel, anthracite nubuck on the mudguard, and embroidered Nike Air branding outlined in red. The insoles read "checked it twice", a nod to Santa's naughty list. The site says it is a darker companion to a red "Santa" Air Jordan 3 for kids. We could not reload the Justfreshkicks page on 20 November, so those material details stay tied to the 11 November report.

Same Saturday, two Jordan 3s

House of Heat's Saturday list does not stop at this pair. It also lists the grade school Air Jordan 3 "Santa" that day. The men's Not Nice List at $215 and a kids' Santa colorway on one date is the split to watch if you are buying more than one size. We do not have a separate retail price from House of Heat for the grade school shoe. Foot Locker's page is the $215 check for the men's pair. Our short card for this colorway is the Not Nice List shoe page.
