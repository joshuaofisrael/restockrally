Nike LeBron XXIV "Stars" debuts November 17 for $180

By the RestockRally desk

Nike says the LeBron XXIV is the lightest LeBron signature shoe yet. The Stars colorway arrives November 17 for $180.

Last updated October 27, 2026

The short answer: the Nike LeBron XXIV launches November 17, 2026 in the debut "Stars" colorway (style code IO8204‑400) for $180, according to Nike's release page, with pricing varying by region.

What is new

Nike says the 24th shoe in LeBron James' signature line uses a new KingKnit upper, a lower profile React midsole and a redesigned midfoot shank. The company calls it the lightest LeBron signature game shoe to date, based on the weight of a men's US size 9 compared with the LeBron 1 through 23, and notes that actual weight can vary by size and colorway.

"LeBron frequently challenges us to continue pushing the limits of what's possible," Nike basketball footwear director Dorian Pickens said in Nike's announcement.

More colorways to follow

Nike says the "Stars" pair has a polarized blue base with black, gold and Racer Blue accents. It also previewed a brushed metallic silver pair, an all yellow pair, a rose toned pair and a multicolor style for Chinese New Year, which Nike says will follow after the debut.
