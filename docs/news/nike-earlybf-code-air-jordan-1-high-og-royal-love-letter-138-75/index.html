The Air Jordan 1 High OG "Royal" just hit $138.75 on Nike, and the code also works on "Love Letter"

By the RestockRally desk

Sneaker accounts on X say Nike's EARLYBF code takes 25% off the Air Jordan 1 High OG "Royal" and "Love Letter", bringing both to $138.75. 9to5Toys confirms the extra 25% code on eligible sale styles.

Last updated November 19, 2026 at 12:20 PM ET

Two of this season's Air Jordan 1 High OG pairs just got cheaper on Nike. "NEW LOW PRICE 25% OFF: Jordan 1 Retro High OG "Royal" $138.75 on Nike," the J23 app posted on X on Thursday, adding that the discount is "automatically applied in cart".

The second pair

Minutes later, SNKR_TWITR posted the same $138.75 price for the Air Jordan 1 High OG "Love Letter" with code EARLYBF, and $89.99 for grade school sizes. Both posts are marked as ads, which means the accounts may earn a commission on sales.

The code behind it

The discount comes from Nike's early Black Friday sale. 9to5Toys reported on Monday that code EARLYBF takes an extra 25% off eligible sale items at checkout, on top of markdowns of up to 50% across hundreds of styles. A price of $138.75 is exactly 25% off $185.

Before you check out

Prices and eligible styles can change during a sale, and the J23 post says the discount was applied in cart for the "Royal" pair, while the "Love Letter" post names the code. Check that the price shows in your bag before you pay. Sizes were not listed in either post. For release news on more Jordans, see our Jordan section.
