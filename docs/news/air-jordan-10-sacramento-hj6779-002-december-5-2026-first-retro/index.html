The Air Jordan 10 “Sacramento” gets its first retro since 1995, and it lands on 5 December for $215

By the RestockRally desk

The Air Jordan 10 "Sacramento" (HJ6779-002) returns for the first time since 1995 on 5 December 2026 for $215 in men's sizes, with grade school pairs also coming. It is listed for Nike SNKRS.

Last updated November 22, 2026 at 9:05 AM ET

One of the rarest original Jordan colourways is finally coming back. Sneaker News posted official images on X of the Air Jordan 10 "Sacramento" on Saturday, and the J23 app also flagged the release.

Why this one matters

The purple-on-black "Sacramento" has not been retroed since the shoe first came out in 1995, Sneaker News says. That makes it a rare first-timer in a holiday calendar full of familiar OG colourways.

It is part of the Air Jordan 10 "City Pack", a set of team-inspired colourways from the original run. The "Bulls" version has returned several times, and the "Seattle" and "Orlando" pairs came back around the turn of the decade. Sneaker News says this "Sacramento" release, restored with tumbled black leather uppers, and a "Knicks" return in 2027 complete modern versions of the original five-pair set.

How to buy

The shoe is listed as a standard release on Nike SNKRS. Nike has not yet confirmed a time. Most US Jordan launches go live at 10am ET, so check the SNKRS app the morning of 5 December. Retailers may also carry pairs.

Our take

First-ever retros of 30-year-old colourways do not come along often. If you want the "Sacramento" at retail, treat 5 December as a real drop, not a casual restock, and have your size and payment ready.
