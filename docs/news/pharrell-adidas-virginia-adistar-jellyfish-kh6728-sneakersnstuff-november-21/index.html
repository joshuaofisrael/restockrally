Pharrell's VIRGINIA Adistar Jellyfish (KH6728) is $300 at one US shop and $329 at another

By the RestockRally desk

Sneakersnstuff lists the Pharrell VIRGINIA Adistar Jellyfish (KH6728) at $300. END. US lists the same code at $329. Checked 21 Nov 2026.

Last updated November 21, 2026

Two US shops are listing the same Pharrell Williams adidas runner under style code KH6728, and they do not charge the same price. Sneakersnstuff US titles it the adidas Originals VIRGINIA Adistar Jellyfish By Pharrell Williams and prices it at $300. END. US titles it the adidas Adistar Jellyfish Sneaker in Onix, Aurora Onix and Core Black and prices it at $329. Both pages were opened on 21 November 2026.

"Virginia adidas Adistar Jellyfish by Pharrell," snkr_twitr posted on X on Saturday morning, in a post marked as an ad, and pointed to Sneakersnstuff US, END. and Slam Jam. The post is an ad, so treat the shop list as a lead and open each product page yourself. Our guide to spotting a fake release ad is the checklist for a link that asks you to pay somewhere else.

What the product pages actually say

Sneakersnstuff lists the article number as KH6728, the gender as unisex, and the color code as ONIX/AUON/CBLACK, which the description spells out as Onix, Aurora Onix and Core Black. The page says the upper is layered textile and synthetic, with a textile lining, a lace up closure, an exaggerated flowing midsole and a sculpted rubber outsole. It also says the shape takes its cue from a jellyfish. That is the store's description, not a line from adidas.

END. lists the same style code and the same three color names. Its spec line adds a TPU cage, torsion support and a rubber outsole, and the product text says the cage glows in the dark. The Sneakersnstuff page does not mention a glow. If that detail matters, the END. page is the one making the claim.

Price, shipping and returns

The $29 gap is on the pages themselves: $300 at Sneakersnstuff and $329 at END. US, same code, same day. An adidas US search for KH6728 on 21 November 2026 did not return a product page for this Adistar Jellyfish, so neither figure is a brand price confirmed on adidas.com. Compare the code on the page before you pay, because a different Jellyfish code is a different shoe.

Sneakersnstuff says US shipping is $5, that orders ship from its Ohio warehouse, and that home delivery is usually 3 to 4 business days, with no custom fees or taxes added. It also says full price items have a 30 day return window, and that sale items are final sale. END. says you can return a purchase within 30 days for an exchange or a refund, and it sends shipping cost and timing to its shipping page rather than printing a dollar figure on the product page.

The Sneakersnstuff size chart is based on men's sizing and shows US 8, 8.5, 9, 9.5, 10, 10.5, 11, 11.5, 12 and 13. It does not list 12.5. A size chart is not a stock count. The guide on the page does not say which of those sizes were available to order.

Slam Jam's list

Slam Jam's upcoming drops page, also opened on 21 November 2026, lists a Pharrell Williams Virginia Adistar Jellyfish in Onix, Aurora Onix and Core Black under KH6728 at €300. That is a euro figure on Slam Jam's list, not a third US price, and the page does not print a US release time next to that code.

Background, and what happened next

KH6728 is not the Jellyfish that release calendars carried for 1 November. Our November 1 to 7 roundup cites SoleInsider for a Pharrell x adidas Adistar Jellyfish at $300 under KH6727, and that code has its own release page. What happened next is this second code, at the shops above, rather than a restock of KH6727. More adidas dates sit on the adidas hub. The same Saturday also has the adidas D.O.N. Issue 8 Solar Turbo. For length, use the adidas size chart, and look a code up in the style code index.
