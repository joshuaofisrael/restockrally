Sneaker releases November 8 to 14, 2026: Kobe 8, Magic Ball AJ11, Air Max 95 and 90

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS pages list a Big Bubble Air Max 95, a Mind 002 Flyknit, the Kobe 8 Shanghai Fireworks, a Magic Ball AJ11, an Air Bakin and two Air Max 90s.

Last updated October 31, 2026 at 7:00 AM ET

The short answer: the second week of November is busy on SNKRS, with launches listed on November 10, 11, 12 and 13. Every price and style code below comes from Nike's own SNKRS product pages, which we opened on October 31, 2026.

Tuesday, November 10

  • Air Max 95 Big Bubble "Cacao Wow and Black" (IX6347‑200), $190. Nike says the upper stacks layers of genuine leather and mesh, with Tropical Teal on the lace loops, branding and Air units.
  • Nike Mind 002 Flyknit "Black and Anthracite" (IR2176‑002), $145. Nike says 22 nodes under the foot move against the sole to engage sensory areas of the brain, and this version uses a Flyknit upper.

Wednesday, November 11

  • Kobe 8 Protro "Shanghai Fireworks" (IO6259‑800), $200, with a Big Kids' listing at $125. More in our Kobe 8 report.
  • Women's Air Jordan 11 "Magic Ball" (IZ1800‑200), $235. More in our Magic Ball report.

Thursday, November 12

  • Air Bakin High SP "Varsity Royal and Black" (IQ5365‑400), $170. Nike says this is only the second time the Air Bakin has come back from the vault, with a ribbed upper, a high padded collar, Max Air in the heel and more Air in the forefoot.

Friday, November 13

  • Air Max 90 "Medium Olive and Sequoia" (IV4504‑201), $170, with a Medium Olive leather upper.
  • Air Max 90 SP "Black" (IV4504‑001), $170, in an all black finish.

Nike says both Air Max 90s keep the Waffle sole and visible Max Air from Tinker Hatfield's original 1990 design. Our release calendar also lists the Nike Air Foamposite One "Phantom" and the Nike KD 19 "Aunt Pearl" for November 13 based on credited calendars; see our November Foamposite guide.

Times

Each SNKRS page we opened showed a 3:00 PM availability time without printing a time zone. SNKRS shows the hour in your own device's time zone, so check the app for your exact time. Nike's upcoming launches list can change, and new pairs are often added during the week. Confirm price and stock on the retailer's page before you buy.
