Air Jordan 11 "Green Screen" set for November 14 at $255

By the RestockRally desk

Nike lists the Air Jordan 11 Green Screen, inspired by Space Jam's green screens, for November 14 at $255.

Last updated October 27, 2026

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS page lists the Air Jordan 11 "Green Screen" (style code IQ5700-900) for November 14, priced at $255.

The story behind the colorway

Nike says the colorway was inspired by the green screens that brought the real world into the cartoon universe of Space Jam. According to the SNKRS listing, Lucky Green covers the familiar silhouette, with dimensional textures and details across the shoe.

Sizes and price

The page shows $255 for the main listing and a second listing at $200; the page does not say which sizes it covers. As with every SNKRS launch, the release hour appears in your local time on the product page.
