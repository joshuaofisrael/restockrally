Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" returns November 7 for $215

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS page lists the Air Jordan 6 White Infrared, style code CT8529-105, for November 7 at $215.

Last updated October 27, 2026

The short answer: the Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" (style code CT8529-105) is listed on Nike's SNKRS launch page for November 7 at $215 for men's sizes.

What Nike says

Nike describes the pair as a bring back of the 1991 shoe, saying the shape, fit and feel mirror the original from 35 years ago. The SNKRS listing credits the design to Tinker Hatfield and says it drew inspiration from German sports cars of the era. Nike also says the colorway was one of the shoe's original launch colorways and that the pair carries Nike Air heel branding.

Price and where to buy

The SNKRS page shows $215 for the main listing, with a second listing on the same page at $155 (the page does not say which sizes it covers); check the size options before release day. SNKRS shows the release time in your local time zone, so open the page from your own device to see the exact hour.
