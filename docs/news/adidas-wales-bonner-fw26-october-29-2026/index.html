adidas Originals by Wales Bonner lists Fall/Winter 2026 for October 29

By the RestockRally desk

Wales Bonner's page sets the Fall/Winter 2026 collection for October 29 at 9:00 AM GMT. adidas lists the Black Pony at $200.

Last updated October 29, 2026 at 11:00 AM ET

The short answer: Wales Bonner's collection page says the adidas Originals by Wales Bonner Fall/Winter 2026 collection launches Thursday, October 29 at 9:00 AM GMT (10:00 CET) on walesbonner.com.

The footwear

The same page says the Karintha uses dark brown crocodile stamped leather with gold brushed eyelets, the Gazelle Snake follows in green, the ADIZERO Adios Suede brings a running shoe into everyday wear, and the BW Pony arrives for the first time in pony hair with lizard print overlays. It also says Harley Weir photographed the campaign, which draws on the attitude of 1980s London.

The Black Pony price and time

adidas.com/us lists the Wales Bonner Karintha BW Army Black Pony Shoes, style code KK2813, at $200, and says they are available Thursday, October 29 at 3:00 pm. That line does not name a time zone. Just Drops, citing the adidas site, lists the same style code for 11:00 AM ET at $200, and says it tracks the pair at nine retailers, eight of them first come, first served and one a raffle. We have not confirmed a raffle on a retailer's own page, so it is not on our raffles page. Enter only through each shop's own website.

Other doors

Footwear Magazine reported on October 28 that the collection goes out on October 29 through adidas.com, the CONFIRMED app and select retailers, and attributed that plan to a brand announcement on October 27. Our Thursday release list already noted two other Wales Bonner pairs from a separate Just Drops tally. The Black Pony is on our release page and in the release calendar. Confirm the hour and the price on the retailer page before you buy.
