Nike Book 2 "Be Legendary" (FZ7013-501) drops Friday 20 November for $145

By the RestockRally desk

Devin Booker's second signature shoe gets the purple Kobe Bryant tribute colourway he first wore on a Kobe 4 PE. It is a general release through Nike and selected retailers.

Last updated November 18, 2026 at 8:40 AM ET

The Nike Book 2 "Be Legendary" is due on Friday, 20 November. It appears on House of Heat's list of this week's releases, and Hypebeast reports it will release in men's sizes for $145 through Nike and selected retailers.

The story behind the colourway

Hypebeast says the purple scheme goes back to 2016, Kobe Bryant's last NBA season, when he gave Devin Booker a signed pair of Kobe 11s inscribed "Be Legendary". Booker later wore the purple look on a Kobe 4 PE during the 2021 NBA Finals and on an exclusive Book 1 PE. Hypebeast describes the Book 2 version as turning that personal tribute into a general release.

What it looks like

The low-top uses light purple tumbled leather on the mudguard and overlays, dark purple ripstop on the upper and a black patent leather Swoosh, with purple laces, a grey speckled midsole and "Chapter Two" lettering on the heel tab, according to Hypebeast. The retail pair does not carry the handwritten "Be Legendary" inscription.

Quick facts

  • Style code: FZ7013-501
  • Colourway: Bright Violet/Black-Wolf Grey
  • Release: Friday 20 November 2026
  • Retail price: $145
  • Where: Nike, plus retailers such as Foot Locker, Champs Sports and Finish Line (SoleDispatch)

Before you buy

Because it is a general release rather than a limited collaboration, it is listed at several big retailers. Release dates can still change, so check the retailer page on the day.
