Nike SB Dunk Low “Ducks of a Feather” dates: The Zoo in November, Shout! in December

By the RestockRally desk

Sneaker News reported on 9 November that Division Street's Ducks of a Feather collection will release The Zoo at Flight Club and on the GOAT app in late November, with Shout! following in December.

Last updated November 11, 2026 at 9:45 AM ET

Division Street's Ducks of a Feather project is returning with two Nike SB Dunk Low colourways, "Shout!" and "The Zoo," and Sneaker News published the staggered release dates on 9 November.

The Zoo is scheduled to launch at Flight Club Tokyo on 20 November, at Flight Club stores in Los Angeles, Miami and New York on 21 November, and on the GOAT app on 26 November, Thanksgiving Day. Shout! follows at Flight Club Tokyo on 4 December, at Flight Club Los Angeles, Miami and New York on 5 December, and on the GOAT app on 10 December. Sneaker News also said both styles will be available through select regional skate shops. (A quoted calendar in the same article lists Tokyo again among the 5 December Flight Club stops; the prose dates above are the ones in the body of the report.)

Sneaker News said the collection connects University of Oregon traditions with sneaker and pop-culture references, in a collaboration with GOAT and Flight Club. Shout! is described as taking cues from the 1978 film National Lampoon's Animal House, which was shot in Eugene and on the Oregon campus, and from the "Shout" tradition at Autzen Stadium. The report says the colourway draws on the 2008 Nike SB Dunk Low "Newcastle," swapping that shoe's blue Swoosh for green, with "SHOUT!" on the heel and other film-related details on the tongue label and insoles.

The Zoo, the report said, refers to the "Autzen Zoo" nickname for the stadium's crowd. It describes croc-embossed materials, faux fur uppers and a snakeskin leather Swoosh, with a stadium sketch and "Once a Duck, Always a Duck" on the sockliner, and a tongue-label ticket graphic numbered 9231967, a reference to 23 September 1967, the day Autzen Stadium opened.

As with earlier Ducks of a Feather projects, Sneaker News said proceeds are intended to benefit participating University of Oregon student-athletes. The article did not list a retail price. Dates and shops can still change, so the Flight Club, GOAT and skate-shop listings closer to each launch remain the places to confirm how to buy a pair.
