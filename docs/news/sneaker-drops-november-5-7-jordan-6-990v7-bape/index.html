Sneaker Drops November 5–7: White Infrared Jordan 6, New Balance 990v7 and Bape x Adidas Lead the Weekend

By the RestockRally desk

Last updated November 4, 2026 at 3:37 AM ET

The November 5–7 sneaker calendar brings together a landmark Air Jordan return, the debut of a new New Balance running-inspired lifestyle model and collaborations spanning basketball, television and streetwear. The busiest day is Saturday, November 7, when the Air Jordan 6 “White Infrared” shares the spotlight with Jordan Brand’s “Black Pack” and a new Bape x Adidas collection.

Release guides published by Complex and House of Heat outline a three-day run that offers options beyond the usual retro basketball launches. Here is what shoppers should watch, organized by scheduled release date.

Thursday, November 5: Crocs, Nike and Space Jam

Thursday’s lineup is broad. House of Heat lists the Harry Potter x Crocs Classic Clog “Hogwarts Express,” Nike Mind 001 releases and restocks, and the Nike Air Force 1 Mid ’07 GORE-TEX Vibram “Black.” The Nike Air Force 1 Low “What The LA” and Nike Air Max 95 GORE-TEX Pack also appear on its November 5 calendar.

Basketball collectors have two more entries to monitor: Nike’s Air Foamposite Pro “College Football” Pack and Jordan Brand’s Space Jam 30th Anniversary Pack. These launches place team-inspired footwear and a film anniversary alongside weather-oriented Nike styles.

Complex separately highlights the Sky High Farms x On Cloud Low WP SHG, scheduled for Thursday at $240 through Complex. The laceless, slip-on shoe uses tooling from the Cloud Hi, making it a distinctly different proposition from the week’s Jordan retros.

Friday, November 6: New Balance 990v7 Makes Its Entrance

The standout Friday release is the New Balance 990v7. Complex lists a $200 price and availability through NewBalance.com and select retailers. The first release uses grey mesh and suede, with blue trim along the Encap midsole. Unlike the returning Jordan colorways, this is the introduction of a new model in an established series.

House of Heat’s Friday calendar also includes the Nike Air Max Goadome Low Foam “Green Goblin,” Nike Book 2 “Desert Night,” Nike ACG Air Max Foamposite “Eggplant,” Adidas Anthony Edwards 3 “With Love,” and a Stranger Things x Puma collection. That mix gives shoppers basketball signatures, unconventional Foamposite-derived designs and an entertainment collaboration to consider on the same day.

Saturday, November 7: Jordan Retros Take Center Stage

Saturday’s headline is the Air Jordan 6 “White Infrared,” priced at $215 according to Complex. The shoe returns with white leather, infrared midsole accents and “Nike Air” heel branding, which Complex says has not appeared on this colorway since 1991. Complex lists Nike SNKRS and select retailers among purchase channels.

Jordan Brand is also releasing the Air Jordan 15 and Air Jordan 17 “Black Pack.” Complex prices each pair at $240 and describes black-based designs with exotic leather trim. The two models provide a more understated alternative to the White Infrared’s recognizable color blocking.

Bape and Adidas round out the major Saturday announcements. Their collection, priced from $60 to $240 in Complex’s guide, draws on late-1990s and early-2000s basketball and streetwear. The range includes the Crazy Energy+ shoe and accompanying apparel, with Complex identified as a release destination.

What to Check Before Release Day

The two calendars agree on the major November 6 and 7 launches, while House of Heat tracks additional releases on November 5. Prices and purchasing channels above are included only where specified by Complex; the other calendar entries should not be assumed to share those prices or retailers.

With multiple models scheduled across three consecutive days, shoppers can use the linked release guides to identify their priorities and check individual retailer listings before launch. Dates and availability can change, and a scheduled release does not guarantee stock in every size or region.
