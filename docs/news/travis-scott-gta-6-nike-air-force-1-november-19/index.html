Travis Scott x GTA 6 Nike Air Force 1 Low expected 19 November at $130, release method still unconfirmed

By the RestockRally desk

Sneaker News says the Travis Scott, Rockstar Games and Nike Air Force 1 pack is expected on 19 November, launch day for Grand Theft Auto 6, with raffles likely. Nike has not confirmed the date.

Last updated November 13, 2026 at 8:55 AM ET

The Travis Scott x Rockstar Games x Nike Air Force 1 Low "GTA 6" pack is lined up for Thursday, 19 November, the same day Grand Theft Auto 6 launches. In a post on X, Sneaker News said the shoes are expected that day, that the release method is not confirmed yet and that raffles are likely. Nike and Rockstar have not announced the date themselves, and GOAT's release guide says the same.

Colourways and price

Sneaker Bar Detroit reports three colourways listed under the Air Force 1 Low '07 RS name: White/Fire Pink, Black/Fire Pink, and Multi-Color/Pale Ivory/Black/Bold Berry/Fire Pink, each at $130. Its first look at the black pair shows a Vice City map debossed on the upper, a money band around the toe box and a pink six-star wanted level on the outsole.

The pair you can't buy

Hypebeast reports that Scott revealed a separate Friends and Family version on Instagram, in black patent leather with camo ripstop panels, pink overlays, an oversized white reverse Swoosh and "GTA" in place of the usual "AIR" on the midsole. That pair is being gifted rather than sold. Hypebeast says the other colourways release on 19 November for $130.

How to prepare

With no official method yet, the safest plan is to watch for a SNKRS listing and for Travis Scott's own site, and to expect raffles at selected stores rather than first come, first served. Prices and dates can still change before launch, so check back before 19 November.
