adidas x SP5DER F50 Formotion releases 14 November at $200 in two neon colourways

By the RestockRally desk

adidas Originals and SP5DER launch the F50 Formotion globally on 14 November via adidas CONFIRMED and selected stores, after a 13 November pre-launch on KINGSPIDER.CO.

Last updated November 13, 2026 at 8:25 AM ET

adidas Originals and SP5DER are releasing their new F50 Formotion collection worldwide on Saturday, 14 November, with a pre-launch today, 13 November, on KINGSPIDER.CO. According to Footwear Magazine, the brands named adidas CONFIRMED, selected retailers and selected adidas stores as the global launch channels.

The shoes

The F50 Formotion SP5DER comes in two colourways: red with pink accents, and green with black accents. It mixes references to the Adizero F50 III football boot with running tooling, using zig-zag mesh, leather overlays, lenticular badges and metal hang tags. Footwear Magazine says the brand announcement prices the footwear at €200, and the US adidas release calendar lists both colourways at $200 for 14 November.

Time and method

Just Drops lists both pairs, the "Supplier Colour" and "Solar Green", for Saturday at 10:00 a.m. ET (7:00 a.m. PT), at $200. When we checked on Friday morning it showed raffles at two stores and first come, first served at 10 or 11 stores, depending on the colourway. CONFIRMED launches are often draws, so open the app ahead of time.

The rest of the collection

The drop also includes black and pink tracksuits, graphic T-shirts, a Cordura backpack and three balaclava colourways, with announced prices from €70 to €140. The collection is also on show at a Bergdorf Goodman pop-up until 23 November, Footwear Magazine reports.
