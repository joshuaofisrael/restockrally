Air Jordan 11 "Green Screen": where to buy on November 14

By the RestockRally desk

The Air Jordan 11 "Green Screen" (IQ5700-900) is listed on Nike SNKRS for November 14, 2026 at $255. The stores named so far, including first come, first served shops.

Last updated November 12, 2026 at 11:32 AM ET

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS page lists the Air Jordan 11 "Green Screen" (IQ5700-900) for November 14, 2026 at $255. A second listing on the same page is $200 for the same date.

Release details

Nike says the pair is "inspired by the green screens that brought the real world into the cartoon universe of Space Jam", with Lucky Green across the upper. SoleDispatch lists the colorway as Multi-Color/Gym Green-Lucky Green-Orange Peel. JustFreshKicks says the date is one day before the film's 30th anniversary, and describes a suede and nubuck upper with a glow in the dark translucent outsole.

Shops to check

  • Nike SNKRS, per Nike's page.
  • Foot Locker: Sole Retriever says the Foot Locker link goes live at 10:00 AM ET on November 14.
  • JD Sports and Finish Line: JustFreshKicks lists both as first come, first served at 10:00 AM ET, and says JD Sports ran an exclusive shipping access window on November 11.
  • Champs Sports, listed by SoleDispatch.
  • ONENESS Boutique: the store's own post says first come, first served in store on November 14, with no raffle and no online hold.

How to buy

Have your size saved before the morning; our Nike sizing guide covers Jordan fit. Read how SNKRS launches work and how restocks work at Nike, Foot Locker and Finish Line in case pairs come back. Enter only on a store's own site or app, and never pay to enter a raffle.

The pair is on our release calendar and the Jordan hub. Confirm details on the retailer's own page before release day.
