Kith x New Balance 2011 "Monuments" collection releases November 9

By the RestockRally desk

Ronnie Fieg's New York-inspired three-pair 2011 pack, in Park, Garden and Banner colorways, drops today at Kith and Kith.com. Garden is an in-store exclusive at New Balance at Kith West Hollywood.

Last updated November 9, 2026 at 8:00 AM ET

Kith and New Balance are releasing a three-pair "Monuments" collection on the New Balance 2011 today, November 9, according to House of Heat. The pack is inspired by New York City.

Where to buy

- Park and Banner: all Kith stores and Kith.com. - Garden: Kith.com, and in store only at New Balance at Kith West Hollywood.

House of Heat did not list retail prices, so check Kith's product pages for pricing and size availability.

About the 2011

Kith launched the New Balance 2011 earlier this year for its 15th anniversary. It is an original silhouette designed by Ronnie Fieg and New Balance. The multi-panel upper sits on ABZORB tooling taken from the New Balance 2002. The model's name refers to 2011, the year Kith was founded and its partnership with New Balance began.

The three colorways

- Park: builds on the palette of Fieg's 2014 New Balance 530. It is mostly maroon with Atomic Yellow and off-white accents, in pigskin suede and microperforated nubuck with quilting across the vamp and collar. - Garden: inspired by flower gardens. It pairs light grey with Amazon Green, lilac, Atomic Yellow and fuchsia, using microperforated pigskin suede, solid suede overlays and leather inserts. - Banner: a Princess Blue pair with silver and orange accents. It has a mesh vamp and pigskin suede overlays.

Details

All three pairs have asymmetrical Kith and New Balance heel branding. They come in custom packaging illustrated with a map of New York City and its monuments, with matching artwork on the sockliners and two extra sets of laces.

Fieg has also teased a fourth colorway, which House of Heat expects to stay a friends-and-family exclusive.
