Air Jordan 14 "Forest Green" returns October 31 for $215

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS page lists the Air Jordan 14 Forest Green, style code 487471-300, for October 31 at $215, its first return since 2005.

Last updated October 30, 2026 at 11:30 London

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS page lists the Air Jordan 14 "Forest Green" (style code 487471‑300) for Saturday, October 31 at $215, with a second listing on the same page at $155.

What Nike says

Nike calls the Air Jordan 14 the last signature shoe Michael Jordan wore on court for Chicago, and says this is the first time the "Forest Green" colorway has returned since its original 2005 release. The listing says the pair keeps chrome details and carbon fiber accents.

Price, sizes and time

The $215 listing is the men's pair. Nike's upcoming launches list also shows a Big Kids' version for October 31, which matches the second $155 listing on the product page. The page we opened on October 30, 2026 read "Available 10/31 at 2:00 PM" with no time zone printed, and SNKRS shows the hour in your own device's time zone. The SoleInsider October calendar lists the same style code and $215 price for October 31.

The pair is on our Air Jordan 14 "Forest Green" release page and in the release calendar. For the rest of November's Jordan lineup, see every Air Jordan releasing in November 2026. Confirm price and time on the retailer's page before you buy.
