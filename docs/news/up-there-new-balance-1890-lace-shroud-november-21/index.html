Up There gives the New Balance 1890 a removable lace shroud, with a Melbourne release party on 21 November

By the RestockRally desk

The Australian retailer's collab is the first 1890 with a lace shroud, over a steel grey upper with blue and red N logos. Price and wide release date are not yet confirmed.

Last updated November 17, 2026 at 9:20 AM ET

Melbourne store Up There has a New Balance 1890 collaboration on the way. Sneaker News posted a first look on X on Monday 16 November, saying the pair brings "a removable lace shroud to the mashup silhouette".

What it looks like

According to Sneaker News, this is the first time a lace shroud has appeared on the 1890. The shroud attaches by lacing through a run of eyelets and can be removed completely, leaving a steel grey upper with alternating blue and red "N" logos. Up There's trademark green shows up on the tongue and insoles.

Release details

Up There is hosting a release party for the shoe in Melbourne on 21 November. Sneaker News says it is not clear whether that will also be the wide release date. No price or style code has been published yet, and we will add them when Up There or New Balance confirms them.

Background

The 1890 has had collaborations with Joe Freshgoods, Action Bronson and JJJJound, but outside partner releases have slowed since it moved into the inline range, Sneaker News notes. Up There has worked with New Balance every year in the fourth quarter since its 2002R in 2022.
