Nike Mind 002 Flyknit Black/Anthracite hits SNKRS 10 November

By the RestockRally desk

Nike SNKRS lists the Mind 002 Flyknit “Black and Anthracite” (IR2176-002) for 10 November 2026 at $145, with Nike’s 22-node Mind outsole under a perforated Flyknit upper.

Last updated November 7, 2026 at 7:00 AM ET

Nike has locked a 10 November 2026 SNKRS date for the Mind 002 Flyknit “Black and Anthracite,” priced at $145.

Listing details

The Nike Launch page shows Black and Anthracite, SKU IR2176-002, $145.00, available 11/10 at 3:00 PM as printed on the page. Confirm the local SNKRS countdown for your region when the draw or first-come window opens. Timezone labels on Launch pages can vary by market, so the on-page clock is the authority over secondary calendars.

Hypebeast reported the same 10 November date and $145 MSRP on 5 November, noting the colorway as Black/Anthracite/Dark Smoke Grey after earlier previews that only carried a loose fall window. That report is useful confirmation that Nike moved the pair from a vague seasonal slot onto a hard Launch date.

What Mind technology claims

Nike’s product copy says the Mind Science Department developed 22 outsole nodes that move against the foot to activate sensory areas of the brain, with stretchy footbed fabric giving the nodes room to travel and soft foam in the midsole for cushioning. The Flyknit upper adds engineered perforations for breathability. Those are Nike’s marketing and science claims on the product page, not independent clinical results reviewed here.

The Mind line sits in Nike’s lifestyle rather than performance-racing lane. Buyers should expect a cushioned daily trainer silhouette with an unusual underfoot feel, not a racing flat or a classic Air Max stack.

Why it matters this week

With the Air Jordan 6 “White Infrared” already covered on Restock Rally’s release-day pages, the 10 November SNKRS pair of Mind 002 Flyknit and Air Max 95 Big Bubble fills the next confirmed Nike Launch window. Neither listing guarantees stock in every size; treat SNKRS as the inventory source of truth on drop day.

Drop-day checklist

On 10 November, treat SNKRS as the live inventory source. Screenshots from resellers do not reserve a pair. If Nike runs a draw, entering early does not guarantee a win; if it runs a first-come window, have payment and shipping details saved beforehand. The $145 price is the Launch-page retail; taxes and shipping are separate.
