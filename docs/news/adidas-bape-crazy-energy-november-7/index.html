Adidas and BAPE set November 7 launch for Crazy Energy+ collaboration

By the RestockRally desk

The latest adidas and BAPE collection pairs the Crazy Energy+ basketball shoe with apparel inspired by late-1990s and early-2000s basketball culture.

Last updated November 7, 2026 at 7:20 AM ET

Adidas and streetwear label BAPE are releasing a new collaborative collection on November 7, including a BAPE take on adidas' Crazy Energy+ basketball shoe. (source)

Adidas said the collection draws inspiration from basketball culture and streetwear in the late 1990s and early 2000s. The collaboration combines a performance basketball silhouette with apparel and accessories featuring BAPE's recognisable design language.

The apparel range includes a varsity jacket with purple accents and a chenille back graphic, a reversible camouflage hoodie, track pants, a warm-up top, a BABY MILO T-shirt, a beanie and a basketball bag.

The brand's announcement says the collection will be available through the adidas CONFIRMED app, selected retailers, BAPE stores and BAPE's website. Adidas' release announcement confirms the November 7 date but does not provide a single universal launch time or price for every item.

Just Drops lists the Crazy Energy+ BAPE shoe among the day's scheduled sneaker releases, with a reported retail price of $200. Buyers should check adidas and participating retailers for current stock, sizing and checkout details.

The release adds another basketball-focused collaboration to the brands' shared catalogue, with the shoe and clothing range aimed at fans of both performance footwear and streetwear.
