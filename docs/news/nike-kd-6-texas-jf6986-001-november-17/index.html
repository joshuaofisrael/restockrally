Nike KD 6 "Texas" returns 17 November for $135

By the RestockRally desk

The 2013 colourway honouring Kevin Durant's one college season at Texas comes back in black, Urban Orange and glow-in-the-dark green, style code JF6986-001.

Last updated November 15, 2026 at 8:50 AM ET

The Nike KD 6 "Texas" (style code JF6986-001) returns on Tuesday 17 November for $135 through SNKRS and Nike Basketball retailers, Sneaker Bar Detroit reports. Just Drops lists the US SNKRS launch at 10:00 AM ET.

The colourway

The pair first released on 10 October 2013 and nods to Kevin Durant's single college season with the Texas Longhorns. SneakerFiles describes a black Hyperfuse upper with Urban Orange across the throat and medial side and Green Glow speckles. Nike's launch page says the green accents glow in the dark, including on the visible Air sole and outsole, and that the retro keeps the original's lightweight synthetic upper and Flywire cables.
