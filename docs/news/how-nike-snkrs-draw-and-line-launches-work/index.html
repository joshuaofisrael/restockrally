How Nike SNKRS Draw and Line launches work

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS 101 page explains Draw, a random pick after entries close, and Line, which works like a queue.

Last updated October 29, 2026 at 19:00 London

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS 101 page describes two launch mechanics. A Draw picks winners at random after the entry window closes. A Line works like a queue: the earlier you join, the more likely you are to find your size.

Draw

Nike says the winner selection happens once the entry window closes, and members are then told if they got the pair. The page uses the words "GOT 'EM" for that notice.

Line

Nike compares a Line to standing in line at a store. Members are told shortly after they enter whether they got the pair.

The names on US launch lists

Just Drops, on a SNKRS calendar updated October 28, 2026, uses its own labels. It describes Let Everyone Order, or LEO, as a line: enter when the launch opens, and the earlier you join, the better your chance at a pair. It describes DAN as a draw: enter while the window is open, and buyers are picked at random after it closes. Nike's page calls the same ideas Line and Draw. Just Drops lists today's SHAI 001 Lace Up "Flare" as a Let Everyone Order launch.

Exclusive Access

On the same SNKRS 101 page, Nike says Exclusive Access is an invitation to products outside regular launches. Just Drops says those offers are tied to an account and are not announced ahead of time, so a public calendar cannot list them early.

Nike's page says to save the correct size and shipping address on the SNKRS account before a launch. Our raffles page explains draws in general, and the release calendar lists dates and prices. This is a description of how the launches work, not a recommendation to buy. The retailer page has the final word on time, price and method.
