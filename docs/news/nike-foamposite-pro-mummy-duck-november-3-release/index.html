Nike Foamposite Pro “Mummy Duck” Leads November 3 Release Calendar

By the RestockRally desk

Nike’s $230 Foamposite Pro Mummy Duck is scheduled for a November 3 SNKRS launch as the week’s footwear calendar accelerates.

Last updated November 3, 2026 at 3:00 PM ET

Nike's Air Foamposite Pro “Mummy Duck” is the headline sneaker scheduled for Tuesday, November 3, according to the JustDrops release calendar. The listing gives a 10 a.m. Eastern launch, a $230 retail price and style code IZ7806-001. It identifies Nike SNKRS as the launch channel, with the LEO, or Let Everyone Order, format, and also lists a first-come, first-served option at one store. Those details are important because the methods describe how customers may attempt a purchase, not whether any given size will remain available.

The release follows Halloween rather than landing on the holiday itself. That timing puts the Mummy Duck in an unusual position: its seasonal styling arrives just as retailers and buyers are shifting towards November's larger holiday calendar. House of Heat's November 2–7 roundup calls the Tuesday release “Mummy Duck 2”, while JustDrops uses the shorter Mummy Duck name. The shared date and model family establish the central event, but shoppers should check the official product listing for the exact naming and size range before ordering.

The Foamposite Pro is not the only Nike basketball-adjacent product on the day's calendar. JustDrops also lists a Nike College Air Max 90 in Ohio State University Red and Black, style JA2109-600, at $140, also timed for 10 a.m. Eastern on SNKRS. That simultaneous scheduling creates a practical decision for shoppers who are interested in both releases: separate listings and launch mechanics should be checked rather than assuming a single checkout process covers them.

JustDrops counts 15 sneaker releases on November 3 and says there are no Jordan Brand releases that day. Its next listed Jordan release is the Air Jordan 1 Low G Spiked “Space Jam” on Thursday, November 5, priced at $175 and scheduled for 10 a.m. Eastern. The distinction is useful for buyers planning their week: Tuesday's main attraction is a Nike Foamposite, while Jordan-related activity becomes more prominent later.

The Foamposite's price also sets it apart from the $140 college Air Max 90. A $90 difference is substantial for customers deciding whether to pursue a themed release or a more conventional retro running silhouette. Neither price is evidence of resale value, and a launch listing alone cannot establish demand. Any resale premium would depend on actual transactions after pairs reach customers, not on pre-release commentary.

Nike's LEO system also should not be confused with a guarantee of instant fulfilment. The format describes an order-entry process; customers still need to check the SNKRS app for launch conditions, eligible sizes, geographic restrictions and order confirmation. Likewise, the single first-come, first-served store mentioned by JustDrops does not mean broad physical availability. The calendar does not establish a nationwide store rollout.

The broader November 3 significance is therefore a specific release, at a specific time and price, inside a week with unusually concentrated launches. Customers seeking the Mummy Duck should verify the product code and Nike listing rather than relying on secondary-market speculation or assuming that a post-Halloween launch is a restock. As of the cited calendar update, this is a scheduled primary release; no verified sell-out or replenishment has been established by these sources.
