Restocks November 20, 2026 at 9:05 AM ET

The G-Unit G6 sold out instantly this summer, and the original colourway is back today for $130

By the RestockRally desk

Reebok and 50 Cent are restocking the G-Unit G6 "OG" in white, blue and red today, 20 November, at 10am ET via Reebok.com, Foot Locker and select retailers for $130, after the summer release sold out.

Last updated November 20, 2026 at 9:05 AM ET

If you missed the G6 in the summer, today is your second chance. The @snkr_twitr account flagged the G-Unit x Reebok G6 "White/Blue/Red" on Friday morning as retailers began listing it.

Time, price and where

The Source says the shoe returns today, Friday 20 November, at 10am ET through Reebok.com, Foot Locker and select retailers, priced at $130. Sizes run from 3.5 to 13 in unisex sizing, plus a 14. Nice Kicks lists the same date and price, style code 100289469, and says the pair sold out straight away when it first dropped this summer.

The shoe

This is the original red, white and blue colourway from 2003. The Source describes a white leather and suede upper, a rubber midsole and an icy outsole.

"The people showed us they still want the G-Unit. After seeing how quickly the first drop moved, we knew we had to bring back the OG colorway. This one is for the day-one fans and everyone who missed out the first time," 50 Cent said, according to The Source.

Tips for today

Restocks of shoes that sold out quickly tend to go fast too. Have your account, address and payment saved on Reebok.com and Foot Locker before 10am ET, and check one retailer at a time rather than refreshing several. The @snkr_twitr post links to DTLR and Shoe Palace listings, which are affiliate links, so prices and stock there may differ.
