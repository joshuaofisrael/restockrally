Air Jordan 6 “White Infrared” Returns November 7 With $215 Retail Price

By the RestockRally desk

Jordan Brand has scheduled the White Infrared Air Jordan 6 for November 7 in full-family sizing, according to sneaker release coverage.

Last updated November 3, 2026 at 3:01 PM ET

The Air Jordan 6 “White Infrared” is scheduled to return on Saturday, November 7, giving Jordan Brand a heritage-focused launch at the end of a busy first week of November. Sneaker News lists a $215 men's retail price, style code CT8529-105 and a Nike SNKRS launch. Its November Jordan calendar says the release will cover full-family sizes, an important detail for shoppers seeking sizes beyond the standard men's range.

This is a return rather than a newly invented colourway. Sneaker News notes that the White Infrared version was last seen in 2014. The intervening period helps explain why this release has a different appeal from the newer designs and entertainment collaborations also scheduled for the week. Buyers are being offered another opportunity to purchase a recognisable colourway through retail channels, rather than being directed solely to older pairs in the resale market.

The precise colourway should not be confused with every other Air Jordan 6 using infrared accents. “White Infrared” is the name given in the cited calendars, and the style code provides a more dependable identification when comparing retailer listings. That matters because sneaker marketplaces and social posts often shorten colour names, which can make distinct editions look interchangeable in search results.

The release sits next to two other Jordan products on November 7. Sneaker News lists an Air Jordan 15 “Black Pack” with style code IZ2585-001 and a $240 retail price, while its weekly roundup also includes an Air Jordan 17 Low from the same Black Pack grouping. These launches offer a clear contrast: one is a retro Jordan 6 colourway, while the Black Pack draws attention to other numbered models in the Jordan catalogue. A customer considering multiple pairs should verify each listing independently.

November's wider Jordan schedule is closely tied to Space Jam. The November 5 calendar includes an Air Jordan 11 MCS “Space Ball” baseball cleat, priced at $225, plus several other Space Jam-inspired Jordan designs. Later in the month, Sneaker News lists the women's Air Jordan 11 “Magic Ball” for November 11 and “Green Screen” for November 14. The White Infrared Jordan 6 therefore provides a more traditional retro alternative amid a month shaped heavily by movie-related releases.

The November 7 launch is also distinct from the Air Jordan 4 “Bred” release currently listed for November 27. That later pair has a reported $230 retail price and is scheduled for full-family sizing. Customers planning purchases across the month face separate launch dates and potentially different retailer participation, not one consolidated Jordan event.

As with all advance calendars, the date and price should be checked against the official retailer listing before purchase. Sneaker News lists SNKRS as a coming-soon channel, which is not proof that every size will be available at a particular time or that stock will be plentiful. The cited reporting does not quantify production, raffle odds or expected resale prices.

The key takeaway for collectors is specific: a White Infrared Air Jordan 6, identified by CT8529-105, is scheduled for November 7 at $215 in men's sizing, with family sizes also expected. Any claim about sell-outs or restocks would require subsequent inventory evidence, and should not be inferred from the prominence of the launch alone.
