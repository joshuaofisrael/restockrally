Nike Air Max "Batman" pack releases 15 November: Air Max 95, Air Max Plus and Air Max 90

By the RestockRally desk

Three Air Max models in black, grey and yellow land on 15 November through Nike.com and selected retailers. The Air Max 95 and Air Max Plus are $190 and the Air Max 90 is $140.

Last updated November 15, 2026 at 9:00 AM ET

Nike's Air Max "Batman" pack is out today, Sunday 15 November. Sneaker News flagged the drop on X on Saturday, writing that the pack "hits stores tomorrow". Sneaker Bar Detroit says it releases through Nike.com and selected retailers.

What is in the pack

The pack covers three models in black, grey and yellow, with grey suede, black mesh and yellow Swooshes across the line-up, according to Sneaker Bar Detroit:

  • Air Max Plus "Batman", style code DM0032-045, with a Smoke Grey cage over black mesh and yellow on the Swooshes and Air units, $190.
  • Air Max 90 "Batman", style code IM9616-002, almost entirely black with yellow on small branding details and the Air units, $140.

Not a DC collaboration

The "Batman" name is a nickname. Sneaker Release Dates notes that it is unofficial and that the shoes are not an official DC Comics collaboration; the black, grey and yellow colours are what earn the label.

Stock and sizes vary by retailer, so check Nike and your local stores before heading out.
