Air Jordan 4 "Bred" returns November 27 for $230

By the RestockRally desk

The Air Jordan 4 Bred is listed for November 27, 2026 at $230, returning with its original materials.

Last updated October 28, 2026

The short answer: the Air Jordan 4 OG "Bred" (style code IX8180‑001) is listed for Friday, November 27, 2026 at $230, according to Footwear News and SoleInsider.

What is different this time

Footwear News notes that last year's "Remastered" version swapped the Durabuck upper for leather, and says this release returns with the shoe's original materials. The outlet also points out that Jordan Brand usually saves one of its biggest releases of the year for Black Friday, and this year that slot belongs to the "Bred."

Release time and price

SoleInsider shows a 10 AM ET start and a $230 retail price. Expect the pair on Nike SNKRS and at Jordan Brand stockists; many shops sell limited Jordans by raffle or in a first come, first served drop, so check each store's own page for its method.
