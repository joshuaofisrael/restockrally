Sneaker releases today and tomorrow: October 28 and 29, 2026

By the RestockRally desk

The Air Max Ecstasy Plus drops October 28, then a college Air Force 1 pack, Wales Bonner adidas and the New Balance 992 land October 29.

Last updated October 28, 2026

The short answer: Wednesday is led by the Nike Air Max Ecstasy Plus in "Triple Black" and "Metallic Silver" at $180 each, and Thursday brings a four pair college Air Force 1 set at $125 each, according to Just Drops' daily release list.

Wednesday, October 28

Just Drops lists both Air Max Ecstasy Plus colorways for 10 AM ET on the SNKRS app as a Let Everyone Order launch, which means anyone who requests a pair in time can buy one while stock lasts. The women's Nike First Sight Shadow "Cream II" also releases at $145. Sneaker News describes the Ecstasy Plus pair as the start of an Air Max 2000 retro and rebrand.

Thursday, October 29

Just Drops lists Air Force 1 pairs for the University of Georgia, Kentucky, Oregon and Texas at 10 AM ET for $125 each. Wales Bonner adidas pairs follow at 11 AM ET, including the Gazelle Suede "Croc" at $220 and the Adizero Adios Suede "Wonder White" at $200, plus the Nike Alphafly 4 at $280 and the New Balance Made in USA 992 at $199.99.

Sneaker News also reports two returning pairs that day: the Nike Air Max Goadome "Black Pony Hair" and the adidas Adizero Adios Pro Evo 3.
