New Balance Abzorb 1890 Altitude Blue Black listed at $392 before November 13 release

By the RestockRally desk

Resale tracker claqa found the New Balance Abzorb 1890 Altitude Blue Black listed at $392 in every size, $212 over its $180 retail, ahead of its 13 November release.

Last updated November 8, 2026 at 7:00 AM ET

The New Balance Abzorb 1890 "Altitude Blue Black" is listed at $392 in every size ahead of its Friday, 13 November release, according to claqa's 7 November price check. With a $180 retail price, that is $212 over retail.

An early premium on a busy day

The Abzorb is one of seven releases claqa lists for 13 November, a day that also includes two Air Max 90s, the Nike KD 19 "Aunt Pearl" and the Air Zoom G.T. Cut "Black Volt". It contrasts with the Air Max 90 "Triple Black" on the same calendar, which is already listed under retail.

claqa's prices are the lowest listing for a new pair with the original box, by US size. A flat $392 across every size suggests a thin pre-release market rather than settled demand.

A snapshot, not a sale

Asking prices are not completed sales, and pre-release listings can change sharply once pairs ship. The release date and $180 retail come from claqa's calendar; check New Balance and authorised retailers for regional timing and final pricing, and factor in platform fees, shipping and authentication before paying a premium.
