Converse SHAI 001 Lace Up "Flare" is listed for October 29 at $120

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS page lists the SHAI 001 Lace Up Flare, style code A23933C-832, for October 29 at $120.

Last updated October 29, 2026 at 7:00 AM ET

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS page lists the Converse SHAI 001 Lace Up "Flare" (style code A23933C-832) for October 29 at $120.

What the SNKRS page shows

Nike says the lace-up keeps the sculptural form of the SHAI 001 and replaces the zipper with laces. The listing we opened on the morning of October 29, 2026 read "Available 10/29 at 2:00 PM" and did not print a time zone on that line. SNKRS shows the hour in the time zone of the device you use, so open the page on your own phone for the exact time.

Color and launch type

Just Drops, citing Nike's SNKRS launch feed, lists the same style code for 10:00 AM ET (7:00 AM PT) as a Let Everyone Order launch, and names the colorway Sun Glow/White/Sun Glow. Its SNKRS calendar, updated October 28 at 10:15 PM ET, lists the pair the same way. Sneaker News reported on October 27 that the color is called Flare, with orange across most of the shoe and a lighter lace and inner lining, and that the retail pair keeps the Converse star and chevron on the heel.

Where to buy

The SNKRS listing is the Nike launch page. Just Drops also counts one first-come, first-served retailer. The pair is on our SHAI 001 "Flare" release page and in the release calendar. The rest of Thursday's slate is in our October 28 and 29 release list. Dates and prices can change, so confirm on the retailer's page before you buy.
