Corteiz x Nike Shox R419 "Metallic Silver" (IQ9951-001) drops Friday 20 November for $190

By the RestockRally desk

The silver Shox R419 lands on SNKRS at 10 a.m. ET as a Let Everyone Order launch, with Corteiz branding on the tongue and the C-Star logo on the forefoot.

Last updated November 19, 2026 at 9:10 AM ET

Corteiz's silver take on the Nike Shox R419 releases on Friday, 20 November. Just Drops, which tracks Nike's SNKRS launch feed, lists it for 10:00 a.m. ET (7:00 a.m. PT) at $190 on the SNKRS app.

How you can buy it

Just Drops says the SNKRS launch is a Let Everyone Order (LEO) release, which means every entrant who orders during the window is in the draw rather than a first-come, first-served rush. It also tracks the pair at three retailers, one of them first come, first served. Complex lists StockX, Nike SNKRS and selected retailers.

Just Drops says the release date has not changed since Nike first listed it on 6 November.

What it looks like

Complex describes it as a silver version of the Shox R419, with Corteiz branding on the tongue and the London label's C-Star logo on the forefoot. The colourway is Metallic Silver/University Red/Black/Metallic Silver.

Resale so far

On Thursday morning Just Drops recorded a lowest resale ask of $373 on Flight Club, almost double retail. Asks before a release often move once pairs ship, so treat that as a snapshot, not a value.
