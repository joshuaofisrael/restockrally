Nike Air Force 1 Low Premium "What the LA" drops November 5

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS page lists the Air Force 1 Low Premium What the LA, style code CT1117-100, for November 5 at $125, inspired by Los Angeles sports.

Last updated October 31, 2026 at 7:00 AM ET

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS page lists the Air Force 1 Low Premium "What the LA" (style code CT1117‑100) for Thursday, November 5, with a listed price of $125.

The idea

Nike describes the shoe as a celebration of Los Angeles as a sports city. According to the listing, the design draws on the teams that call the city home and mixes materials and colors into a collage, in the style of other "What the" pairs that combine several looks on one shoe. Nike does not name any team on the page, and RestockRally is not affiliated with Nike or any team.

About the Air Force 1

Nike's page recaps the model's history: introduced in 1982, it was the first basketball sneaker to house Nike Air before becoming a street staple.

Time and where to buy

The page we opened on October 31, 2026 read "Available 11/5 at 3:00 PM" with no time zone printed. SNKRS shows the hour in your own device's time zone. November 5 is a busy day: the Air Max Foamdome "Eggplant" and Jordan 11 MCS "Space Jam" are on SNKRS the same day. The pair is in our release calendar. Confirm the price on the retailer's page before you buy.
