How Restock Rally labels this

Nike had not, in the sources checked for this draft, issued its own sell through bulletin for the Meteorite colorway. Stock flashes from Sole Links and similar accounts are useful signals for buyers, but they can lag or lead true site availability by minutes, and affiliate links may push one retailer over another.

If you are hunting a size, treat the Sole Links note as a prompt to check Nike US and Foot Locker yourself rather than as proof that the pair is gone everywhere. Restock windows reopen; chatter posts do not create official Nike scarcity claims on their own.
