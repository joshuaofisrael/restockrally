A$AP Rocky x PUMA Suede '94 in faded navy releases today at $120

By the RestockRally desk

PUMA says the A$AP Rocky Suede '94 in distressed navy launches worldwide on 12 November at PUMA.com, PUMA stores and select retailers. Sneaker News lists style 408735-04 at $120.

Last updated November 12, 2026 at 8:00 AM ET

A$AP Rocky's latest PUMA Suede '94 lands today, Thursday, 12 November. PUMA's announcement says the shoe is available worldwide at PUMA.com, PUMA retail stores and select accounts, in a distressed navy suede.

Price and style code

PUMA's release did not give a price. Sneaker News lists the pair as style 408735-04 at $120 for a 12 November release through PUMA and select retailers. Sneaker News calls the colour faded blue and says it follows an earlier faded black pair in the same retro build.

Design details

PUMA says the shoe returns the Suede to its '90s shape with a raw-edge padded tongue, a gum rubber sole and retro details, finished with a gold PUMA lace keeper and custom branding. A$AP Rocky, who PUMA says has been its creative director for more than three years, said in the release that he carried "the washed effect" throughout to give the pair a worn-in feel. Sneaker News adds that the Formstrip is grey and the midsole white.

The drop comes with apparel: PUMA says Rocky revived its No. 1 logo tee and hoodie from the '90s, with era-specific fit and graphics. Highsnobiety also flags a custom shoebox among the details.

Where to buy

Start with PUMA.com and the PUMA app, then PUMA stores and the retailers PUMA calls select accounts. Release times can differ by region and retailer, so confirm the style code 408735-04 and the price on the product page before checkout.
