Jordan Spizike Low Jam Central arrives for Space Jam anniversary

By the RestockRally desk

Jordan Brand’s Spizike Low Jam Central release is listed at $165, with the colourway tied to the 30th anniversary of Space Jam.

Last updated November 6, 2026 at 8:00 AM ET

Jordan Brand’s Spizike Low “Jam Central” is part of the sneaker releases surrounding the 30th anniversary of Space Jam, with a listed retail price of $165. (Variety)

Variety’s shopping report describes the release as a new colourway of the Spizike Low, a line that brings together design elements from several classic Air Jordan models. The report places the shoe within Jordan Brand’s continuing tribute to filmmaker Spike Lee, whose Mars Blackmon character became associated with Nike and Michael Jordan advertising. The Jam Central edition is linked to the 30th anniversary of the 1996 film Space Jam. HYPE COMPANY also identifies the release as part of the week’s Space Jam anniversary themed footwear drops.

The $165 price is consistent across the Variety shopping report and Nike’s product page. Availability can vary by size and retailer, and a product listing does not guarantee that every size remains in stock.

The Spizike concept combines elements from Air Jordan III, IV, V, VI, IX and XX models. The low cut format gives the anniversary colourway a different profile from the original high top Spizike, while the colour palette connects it to the film’s visual identity.

The release is one part of a wider Space Jam themed period for Jordan Brand. HYPE COMPANY’s weekly drop report also highlights other anniversary related footwear releases scheduled around the same week.
