Kobe 3 Low Protro "Mamba Eterno" and Kobe Dunk Low Protro launch October 30

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS pages list the Kobe 3 Low Protro Mamba Eterno at $190 and the Kobe Dunk Low Protro Anthracite and Sundial at $130 for October 30.

Last updated October 30, 2026 at 7:30 AM ET

The short answer: Nike's SNKRS page lists the Kobe 3 Low Protro "Mamba Eterno" (style code IO6260‑800) for October 30 at $190, and a separate SNKRS page lists the Kobe Dunk Low Protro "Anthracite and Sundial" (IQ3922‑001) the same day at $130.

Kobe 3 Low Protro "Mamba Eterno"

Nike says this colorway nods to the Día de Muertos tradition, which it describes as a celebration connecting generations through honor and hope. According to the listing, decorative graphics run through the TPU cage and upper, with asymmetrical Swoosh logos and Emerald Green and Hyper Grape accents. Nike says the Protro build adds more breathable materials and tongue padding, a full length Air Zoom strobel and a Cushlon 3.0 foam midsole, plus retooled traction that cuts weight.

Kobe Dunk Low Protro "Anthracite and Sundial"

The Kobe Dunk Low Protro page says the shoe uses a drop in ReactX foam midsole and an Anthracite and Sundial leather upper meant for wear off the court. The page shows $130 for the main listing and a second listing at $100; it does not say which sizes the lower price covers, and Nike's upcoming launches list includes a Big Kids' version of the Kobe Dunk Low on the same date.

Time and where to buy

Both pages we opened on the morning of October 30, 2026 read "Available 10/30 at 2:00 PM" without printing a time zone. The SoleInsider October calendar lists the Mamba Eterno for the same date at $190. SNKRS shows the hour in your device's time zone, so open the page on your own phone for the exact time. Both pairs are in our release calendar, and our SNKRS Draw and Line explainer covers how launches work. Dates and prices can change, so confirm on the retailer's page before you buy.
