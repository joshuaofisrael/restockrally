Nike Kobe 9 EM Low Protro "Philippines" returns 18 November for $200

By the RestockRally desk

The 2014 colourway inspired by Filipino basketball culture comes back with Protro tooling in Laser Crimson, Obsidian and Silver, style code IO6262-600.

Last updated November 15, 2026 at 8:40 AM ET

The Nike Kobe 9 EM Low Protro "Philippines" (style code IO6262-600) releases on Wednesday 18 November at 10:00 AM ET for $200, according to Just Drops, which lists it as a Let Everyone Order launch on the SNKRS app. The colourway is Laser Crimson/Obsidian/Silver.

Back for the first time

The colourway first released in 2014 as a tribute to the Philippines' love of basketball, SneakerFiles says, drawing on the country's basketball courts and banig weaving patterns. Sneaker Bar Detroit says this is the first time Nike has brought it back, now on updated Protro tooling, in adult sizes through SNKRS and selected retailers, with grade school sizes also expected.
