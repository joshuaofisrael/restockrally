First look inside the box: the women's Air Jordan 4 "Pink Thunder" lands on 19 December for $220

By the RestockRally desk

An unboxing posted on X shows the women's Air Jordan 4 "Pink Thunder", dropping on 19 December. Nice Kicks lists it at $220 in Black/White/Rust Pink, style HV0823-001, via SNKRS and select retailers.
