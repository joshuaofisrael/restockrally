The Whitaker Group's patchwork adidas Campus 00s just went live on adidas US, and it looks like a hand-repaired pair for $120

By the RestockRally desk

The Whitaker Group x adidas Campus 00s "Do Not Duplicate" in Dash Grey/Clear Onix/Preloved Ink (LA6870) released on adidas US today, 20 November, for $120, after landing early at Social Status.

Last updated November 20, 2026 at 9:43 AM ET

The latest pair from James Whitner's Whitaker Group and adidas is out now. "NOW LIVE via adidas US," KicksFinder posted on X early on Friday, naming The Whitaker Group x adidas Campus 00s "Do Not Duplicate" in "Dash Grey/Clear Onix/Preloved Ink" at $120 with free shipping and returns.

Just Drops lists the same date and price, and says the pair was first seen on sale at Social Status on 14 November, six days before the adidas date. Its store check this morning shows it tracked at three retailers, all first come, first served.

What the shoe looks like

Japanese release site Sneaker Wars describes a patchwork build. The upper mixes grey textiles of different weaves with navy panels, with hairy suede around the toe and denim around the laces. The seams are picked out in zigzag stitching, and the side carries a small red cross and yellow embroidery, so the pair looks patched and repaired rather than brand new.

Inside, the collar is lilac, and the rubber sole is a dusty pink. Thick pale laces, a padded tongue and a metal lace tag marking the collaboration finish it off, Sneaker Wars says.

Why "Do Not Duplicate"

The Whitaker Group runs A Ma Maniére, Social Status, APB and Jaidé. According to Sneaker Wars, Do Not Duplicate is an ongoing project with adidas Originals built on the idea that individuality cannot be copied, and earlier pairs included the Adizero Evo SL and the Japan SQ.

Should you buy now?

Collaboration pairs at $120 tend to sell through quickly in common sizes, and Just Drops says Social Status has had them on sale since 14 November. If you want one, adidas US is the straightforward option. The KicksFinder post uses an affiliate link, so check the price on adidas.com directly.
