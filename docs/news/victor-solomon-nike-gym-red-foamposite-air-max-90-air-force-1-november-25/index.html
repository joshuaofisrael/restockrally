Release Dates November 22, 2026 at 9:00 AM ET

Victor Solomon’s red “trophy” Nikes drop on 25 November, and the Foamposite costs $350

By the RestockRally desk

Victor Solomon's Gym Red collection with Nike releases on SNKRS on 25 November at 10am ET: the Air Foamposite One (IR2077-600, $350), Air Max 90 (IR2074-600, $250) and Air Force 1 '07 (IR2076-600, $250).

Last updated November 22, 2026 at 9:00 AM ET

Victor Solomon is back with Nike, and this time everything is red. The J23 app posted on X on Saturday, in a post marked as an ad, that the Victor Solomon x Nike "Red Trophies" collection releases on 25 November.

The three pairs

Nike's SNKRS launch pages list three shoes, all available on 25 November at 10am ET (3pm UK):

  • Air Foamposite One x Victor Solomon "Gym Red and Black", IR2077-600, $350. Nike says ornate paisley patterns sit on the smooth, sculptural upper, with gold chain laces and lace locks.
  • Air Max 90 x Victor Solomon "Gym Red and White", IR2074-600, $250. The launch page describes pebbled and smooth leather, a Swoosh "loaded with gemstones" and a gold chain in place of standard laces.
  • Air Force 1 '07 x Victor Solomon "Gym Red and White", IR2076-600, $250. Nike lists a dimensional gold Swoosh and gold chain laces with lace locks.

All three carry Solomon's sign-off on the back tab.

Who is Victor Solomon

Solomon is an artist known for his sculptural takes on sports trophies, which is where the "trophy" theme comes from. Nike's copy opens each page with "It's back", pointing to the return of his earlier work with the brand.

How to buy

All three pairs launch on Nike SNKRS. Nike has not said on these pages whether the drop is a draw or first come, first served, so check the app on the morning of 25 November. The Foamposite is the priciest pair at $350, $100 more than the Air Max 90 and Air Force 1.

Our take

Gold chain laces and gemstones are not for everyone, but collabs like this tend to move fast on release day. If you want one pair, decide which before 10am ET, since three launches at once can split traffic in the app.
