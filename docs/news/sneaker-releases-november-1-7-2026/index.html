Sneaker releases next week: November 1 to 7, 2026

By the RestockRally desk

Kobe 3 Low Black, Pharrell's Adistar Jellyfish, a Foamposite college pack, Union's Bamboo Dunk and the Air Jordan 6 White Infrared lead November 1 to 7.

Last updated October 30, 2026 at 11:30 London

The short answer: the first week of November brings a release almost every day, led by the Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" on Saturday, November 7, according to the SoleInsider November calendar and Nike's own SNKRS upcoming list.

Sunday, November 1

SoleInsider lists the Nike Kobe 3 Low Protro "Black" (IV7127‑002) at $190 and the Pharrell x adidas Adistar "Jellyfish" (KH6727) at $300, both at 10 AM ET. Sneaker Freaker's calendar adds New Balance 991v2 "Elevated Neutrals" pairs for the same day.

Tuesday to Thursday

The Air Foamposite Pro "Mummy Duck" is listed for November 3. SoleInsider now shows it at $240 under style code JA5895‑001, while Sneaker Freaker lists style code IZ7806‑001 for that date, so check the code on the retailer page. On November 5, SoleInsider lists the Foamposite Pro "FSU," "LSU" and "Ohio State" at $250 each and the Victor Wembanyama x Nike GT Cut 4 "State of Liberty" at $210. Nike's SNKRS pages list the Air Max Foamdome "Eggplant" at $265 and the Jordan 11 MCS "Space Jam" cleat at $225 the same day.

Friday, November 6

SoleInsider lists the New Balance 990v7 Made in USA (U990GR7) at $200, the Union x Nike Dunk Low "Bamboo" at $130 and the Nike SB Dunk Low "Champagne" (IQ0431‑300) at $130. Nike's SNKRS page lists the One Block Down Air Max 95 Big Bubble Lux at $200.

Saturday, November 7

The Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" arrives at $215, and SoleInsider lists the Nike Book 2 "Chromaflair" (FZ7013‑003) at $145, noting its date moved from November 8. Sneaker Freaker's calendar also lists the Air Jordan 15, an Air Jordan 17 Low and two Bad Bunny x adidas BadBo pairs for November 7.

US clocks go back one hour on November 1, so a 10 AM ET launch lands one hour later in UTC than it did in October. Every pair with a confirmed price is in our release calendar. Dates often move, so confirm on the retailer page before release day.
