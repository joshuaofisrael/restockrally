Nike kids' shoe sizes explained: what C and Y mean on release day

By the RestockRally desk

Nike splits kids' shoes into baby and toddler (1C to 10C), little kids (10.5C to 3Y) and big kids (3.5Y to 7Y). Here is how that maps to adult sizes.

Last updated November 1, 2026 at 5:00 AM ET

The short answer: on Nike and SNKRS, a "C" size is a child size and a "Y" size is a youth size. Nike's kids' shoe guide lists babies and toddlers at 1C to 10C, little kids at 10.5C to 3Y and big kids at 3.5Y to 7Y.

Why it matters on launch day

Many SNKRS launches carry several listings for the same colorway, such as a men's pair and a Big Kids' pair at a lower price. The Air Jordan 14 "Forest Green" page, for example, shows $215 and $155 listings, and Nike's upcoming list names a Big Kids' version. Picking the wrong listing is an easy mistake when a launch moves fast.

Big kids' sizes and adult sizes

Nike's kids' footwear size chart maps big kids' Y sizes directly to US men's sizes: 4Y lines up with men's 4, 5Y with men's 5 and 7Y with men's 7. The same chart lines 7Y up with women's 8.5, and 5Y with women's 6.5. That is why some adults with smaller feet look at Big Kids' listings.

How Nike says to measure

Nike says to measure both feet to the longest point of the toes, use the longer measurement, and size up if you land between sizes. Its fit guide suggests leaving about a fingertip of room at the toe, and says a heel that slips while walking usually means the shoe is too big.

Sizing can vary by model, so check the size guide on the product page before a launch. Our release calendar lists upcoming pairs, and the SNKRS explainer covers how to save your size before a draw.
