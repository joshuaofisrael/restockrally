adidas gave the Samba a houndstooth winter coat, and the Gazelle Indoor and Handball Spezial got one too

By the RestockRally desk

adidas Originals has a winter collection for the Samba, Gazelle Indoor and Handball Spezial with houndstooth and wool-inspired details. Prices and release dates for each pair have not been confirmed.

Last updated November 22, 2026 at 9:10 AM ET

The Samba is getting dressed for winter. Sneaker News posted on X on Saturday that adidas Originals "brings winter style to three of its most iconic sneakers": the Samba, the Gazelle Indoor and the Handball Spezial.

The three pairs

According to SportsOutdoors, each shoe borrows from classic cold-weather clothing:

  • Samba: a deep navy upper with dark red Three Stripes, a houndstooth pattern inspired by wool coats and blazers, and a dark gum sole.
  • Gazelle Indoor: Magic Mauve suede with dark burgundy stripes and a woollen pattern inspired by tailored suit jackets, on a gum sole. This one comes in women's sizes.
  • Handball Spezial: a soft beige suede upper with pecan brown leather stripes and heel tabs, houndstooth detailing and a gum rubber sole.

Price and release date

adidas has not confirmed prices or a single release date for the three pairs in the coverage we checked. SportsOutdoors says availability varies and some options are selling through quickly. Sneaker News' write-up points shoppers to adidas.com and authorised retailers.

Why it matters

The Samba, Gazelle and Handball Spezial have been among the biggest terrace-style sneakers of the past few years. Seasonal takes like these give fans a way to keep wearing them through winter without another plain black-and-white pair. Houndstooth is an unusual touch for a sneaker, and it ties the shoes to tailoring rather than sportswear.

Our take

If you want the Handball Spezial, move quickly, since reports suggest it is going fast. The navy Samba is the easiest of the three to wear daily. We will update this page if adidas confirms prices or a wider release date.
