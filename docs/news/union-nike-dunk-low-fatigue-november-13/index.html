Union x Nike Dunk Low "Fatigue" set for 13 November on SNKRS at $130

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS page lists the Union Dunk Low "Fatigue" in Bamboo and University Gold (IF7380-200) for 13 November at $130. Just Drops says it is a Let Everyone Order launch at 10 a.m. ET.

Last updated November 12, 2026 at 7:40 AM ET

Union's Nike Dunk Low "Fatigue" is the headline release for Friday, 13 November. Nike's SNKRS launch page lists the Bamboo and University Gold pair, style IF7380-200, at $130 with availability on 13 November.

Time and method

Just Drops gives the drop time as 10:00 a.m. ET (7:00 a.m. PT) and lists it as a Let Everyone Order (LEO) launch on SNKRS, which means anyone who checks out while stock lasts can buy, rather than entering a draw. The tracker says Nike moved the date from 6 November to 13 November, and on Thursday morning it showed the shoe at seven retailers, five of them first come, first served.

The design

Nike describes the pair as a take on a daily uniform. It says the deconstructed build drops the traditional lining and adds a collapsible heel, with a suede upper carrying Union's zig-zag stitching and Nike x UNION co-branding. Sneaker Bar Detroit describes the suede as olive with exposed stitching near the toe and a small Union tag, and says the fold-down heel lets the shoe be worn like a mule. It also reports a black pair in the same capsule.

Sizing note

Nike's page says this style runs slightly large and suggests going down half a size for a closer fit. That is worth deciding before the drop, since LEO stock in popular sizes can go quickly.

Where to buy

The main route is the SNKRS app. Union stores and the first come, first served retailers listed by Just Drops are the other options; check each store's page for its own release time.
