Nike lists the Kobe 8 Protro Shanghai Fireworks on SNKRS at $200

By the RestockRally desk

Nike's SNKRS launch page for the Laser Orange and Safety Orange Kobe 8 Protro, SKU IO6259-800, shows a $200 price and a U.S. commerce start of 11 November 2026 at 10:23 a.m. ET.

Last updated November 11, 2026 at 1:00 PM ET

Nike's SNKRS launch page for the Kobe 8 Protro "Shanghai Fireworks" is live, priced at $200 and identified as SKU IO6259-800. Product data on that page lists a U.S. commerce start of 11 November 2026 at 15:23 UTC, or 10:23 a.m. ET, with a current and full price of $200.

Nike says the colorway, in Laser Orange and Safety Orange, originally appeared in 2013 and is now returning as a Protro model. The official description says the upper has "vibrant artwork depicting the brilliance of a fireworks display." The Protro version, Nike says, uses a Nike React foam insert, a soft and pliable synthetic upper, and grippy traction for cuts and acceleration. It is described as low, sleek and light.

The listing reviewed on 11 November is a U.S. SNKRS product page for this SKU at $200. Availability on the app or in stores can change, and a launch-page listing is not a guarantee that a given size remains in stock. Details above are taken from Nike's own SNKRS page.
