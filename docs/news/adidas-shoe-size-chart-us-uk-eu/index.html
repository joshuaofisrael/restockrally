adidas shoe size chart: US, UK, EU and JP sizes explained

By the RestockRally desk

The adidas shoe size chart from US men's 4 to 15 with UK, EU, JP and heel to toe length, how adidas differs from New Balance and Nike, and what to do between sizes.

Last updated November 12, 2026 at 8:04 AM ET

adidas publishes one footwear chart for men and women on its US size chart page. It lines up heel to toe length in inches and centimeters with US men's, US women's, EU, UK and JP sizes. Here is how to read it, and where it differs from Nike's.

Measure first

adidas says to measure your feet at the end of the day for the best fit, from the tip of your big toe to the outermost part of your heel. Match that length to the heel to toe row on the chart, then read down for your size in each system.

Rows copied from adidas's US chart, read 12 November 2026. The chart continues to US men's 20 (35.2 cm, UK 19, EU 55 2/3). US women's sizes stop at 15.5, and JP sizes stop at 325.

Three patterns in the chart

  • US women's is one size above US men's. On the adidas chart a men's 8 sits in the same column as a women's 9. That is different from Nike's charts, which put women's sizes 1.5 above men's. Our Nike sizing guide covers Nike's rule.
  • UK is half a size below US men's. A US men's 9 is a UK 8.5 in every row of the adidas table.
  • EU sizes move in thirds. adidas lists EU sizes such as 40 2/3 and 41 1/3, so an EU number from another brand may not have an exact match.

adidas vs New Balance at the same US size

Brands do not print the same foot length against the same US size. adidas puts US men's 9 at 26.3 cm heel to toe. New Balance's size guide puts US men's 9 at 27 cm, and its EU number is 42.5 rather than adidas's 42 2/3. The two charts also handle women's sizes differently: adidas adds one size (men's 9 is women's 10), while New Balance adds 1.5 (men's 9 is women's 10.5). Use each brand's own chart rather than carrying a number across. Our New Balance sizing and widths guide has the New Balance rows.

Between sizes

adidas's own advice: for a tight fit go one size down, and for a loose fit go one size up. It also says its men's chart follows standard sizing, so it should reflect your normal fit. If a particular product page carries its own fit note, that note is specific to the shoe.
