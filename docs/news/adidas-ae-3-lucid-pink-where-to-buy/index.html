adidas AE 3 "Lucid Pink": where to buy, and why calendars still say November 20

By the RestockRally desk

The adidas Anthony Edwards 3 "Lucid Pink" (KH8545, $130) went on sale early at Foot Locker on October 21, 2026, while some calendars still list November 20. What each source says.

Last updated November 12, 2026 at 11:34 AM ET

The short answer: the adidas AE 3 "Lucid Pink" (KH8545) is a $130 pair that Sneaker News reported "available now at Foot Locker" on October 21, 2026, ahead of the Timberwolves' season opener. Several release calendars still show November 20, so you may see both dates.

What each source says

  • Sneaker News, October 21: available now at Foot Locker for $130 in men's sizes.
  • Sole Retriever: originally slated for November 20, it released on October 21 through adidas, a day after early access on the PrizePicks app. It lists the colorway as Lucid Pink/College Purple/Bliss Lilac, unisex sizing, $130.
  • Just Drops still shows November 20 as expected, from a Finish Line calendar listing, and notes adidas has not confirmed a date.
  • SoleInsider also lists November 20 as an expected date, at adidas CONFIRMED and select retailers.

Our read: the pair has already sold at retail through Foot Locker and adidas, and some retailers may still hold a November 20 slot. Check stock on the store's own page by style code KH8545.

How to buy

  • Search by style code at adidas and Foot Locker first, then Finish Line closer to November 20.
  • adidas drops on the CONFIRMED app work differently from SNKRS; see how the adidas CONFIRMED app works.
  • Check your size on our adidas size chart guide. Sole Retriever says the pair comes in unisex sizing.
  • Never pay to enter a raffle; see how to spot a fake release ad or raffle.
