Jordan Tatum 5 "South Beach" drops today at 10 a.m. ET for $135

By the RestockRally desk

The Light Smoke Grey, Aurora Green and Polarized Pink Tatum 5 (IO1746-003) releases on Thursday, 12 November, at 10 a.m. ET. Nike.com lists it at $135.

Last updated November 12, 2026 at 8:20 AM ET

The Jordan Tatum 5 in a Miami-inspired grey, teal and pink mix is the main Jordan Brand release on Thursday, 12 November. The Just Drops release page lists style code IO1746-003 for 10:00 a.m. ET (7:00 a.m. PT) on Nike.com at $135, and says it was tracking the shoe at eight retailers on Thursday morning, six of them first come, first served.

Price and colorway

Nike's own Tatum 5 product page shows the pair at $135 in men's sizes, in Light Smoke Grey/Aurora Green/Marrakesh/Polarized Pink. Sneaker News quoted $125 when it first previewed the shoe on 30 October, but the Nike listing is the price to go by at checkout.

"South Beach" is a nickname, not Nike's official name. Sneaker Files notes that some retailers call this pair "Supernova." It describes Light Smoke Grey across most of the upper, a darker grey midfoot strap with a rubber Jumpman, teal mesh on the medial side and a pink heel counter shaped like Tatum's number 0.

What Nike says about the shoe

Nike describes the Tatum 5 as a basketball shoe with Cushlon 3.0 foam, a forefoot Air Zoom unit and a new outsole stability strap for lateral support on quick cuts.

Where to buy

Sneaker Files lists Nike, Foot Locker, Hibbett and DSG among the stockists and calls this a general release rather than a limited one. That usually means sizes last longer than on a hyped collaboration, but stock can still vary by store and region. Check the retailer's product page for the style code IO1746-003 before you pay.
