Surprise: the Nike Kobe 10 Protro "Duke" PE (JW6581-001) is getting a public release this holiday season

By the RestockRally desk

Sneaker News flagged it on X. Release trackers list the black and Game Royal pair at $190 through SNKRS and selected retailers, with no exact date yet. A UConn version is expected too.

Last updated November 19, 2026 at 9:15 AM ET

A Duke player exclusive is heading to the public. "Well this is a surprise... The Nike Kobe 10 Protro "Duke" PE will see a public release this Holiday season," Sneaker News posted on X on Wednesday evening.

Price, code and where to buy

Sneaker Files lists the pair as style code JW6581-001 in Black/White-Game Royal, at $190, releasing in Holiday 2026 through select Nike Basketball retailers including SNKRS. Sneaker Bar Detroit also has it at $190 in unisex sizes, in stores and online, during the 2026 holiday season. Neither site gives an exact date, and Nike has not posted a SNKRS page yet.

Which Duke pair it is

Duke has home and road versions of this Kobe 10 PE, but both trackers say only the black pair is expected to release. According to Sneaker Bar Detroit and Sneaker Files, it has:

  • a black mesh upper with white Swooshes outlined in Duke's Game Royal blue
  • blue around the toe that runs onto the front of the midsole and fades to black towards the heel
  • a large white Duke "D" on one heel and Kobe's Sheath logo on the other
  • Kobe's signature on the tongue and again across a glossy blue "X" on the outsole

Both outlets say a UConn Kobe 10 Protro PE is expected alongside it.

Quick facts

  • Style code: JW6581-001
  • Colourway: Black/White-Game Royal
  • Price: $190
  • Release: Holiday 2026, date to be confirmed
  • Where: SNKRS and selected Nike Basketball retailers
