Nike SB Air Force 1 Low "Navy Black" drops 17 November for $120

By the RestockRally desk

Style code HM8517-400 launches on SNKRS at 10:00 AM ET on Tuesday as a Let Everyone Order release, after Nike moved it from 13 November. One store has had pairs since 14 November.

Last updated November 16, 2026 at 8:50 AM ET

The Nike SB Air Force 1 Low in Navy/Navy/Black (style code HM8517-400) releases on Tuesday 17 November at 10:00 AM ET (7:00 AM PT) for $120, according to Just Drops, which cites Nike's SNKRS launch feed.

How to buy

Nike lists it as a Let Everyone Order launch on the SNKRS app, so anyone who enters during the window gets a pair rather than going into a draw. Just Drops tracks it at four retailers, two of them first come, first served. Its daily calendar names it the lead release among 16 shoes dropping on Tuesday.

A date change and an early store

Nike moved the release from 13 November to 17 November, a change Just Drops says it detected on 5 November. The site also reports that Premier has had the shoe on sale since 14 November, three days before the brand date.

Price check

Just Drops showed the lowest resale ask at $116 on GOAT on 16 November, slightly below retail. That suggests little resale premium, though asks can move once SNKRS stock sells.
