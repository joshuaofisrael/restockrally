Air Max 90 Triple Black already listed below $165 retail ahead of November 13 release

By the RestockRally desk

Resale tracker claqa found the Nike Air Max 90 Triple Black listed under its $165 retail in every size from 8 to 12 before its 13 November release, from $131.

Last updated November 8, 2026 at 7:20 AM ET

The Nike Air Max 90 "Triple Black" is already listed for less than its $165 retail price ahead of its Friday, 13 November release, according to claqa's 7 November price check. The resale tracker found listings under retail in every US size from 8 to 12, starting at $131.

Falling before launch

A week earlier the cheapest listing was $161, claqa said, so the lowest asking price has dropped about 19% in seven days. Its figures are the lowest listing for a new pair with the original box, by US size, and pre-release prices can move quickly.

A busy Friday

The Triple Black is one of two Air Max 90s on claqa's 13 November calendar, alongside the "Medium Olive Sequoia" at $170, which had no listings yet. The same day also brings the Air Diamond Turf "Patent Black" ($170), the New Balance Abzorb 1890 "Altitude Blue Black" ($180), the Nike KD 19 "Aunt Pearl" ($170) and the Air Zoom G.T. Cut "Black Volt" ($190), among others.

What it means for buyers

claqa's editor wrote that anyone who wants the Triple Black in sizes 8 to 12 could buy now, since it is already under retail in all of them. Asking prices are not completed sales, and fees, shipping and tax can close the gap with a retailer checkout. Check current listings and the retail release page before you buy.
