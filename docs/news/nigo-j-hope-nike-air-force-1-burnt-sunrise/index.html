Sole Links: NIGO x J-Hope Air Force 1 '01 down to a few sizes at End

By the RestockRally desk

Sole Links flagged on 8 November 2026 that the NIGO x J-Hope x Nike Air Force 1 '01 in Burnt Sunrise (style code IO8766-800) was down to a few sizes at End.

Last updated November 8, 2026 at 8:30 AM ET

The NIGO x J-Hope x Nike Air Force 1 '01 was down to a few sizes at End on Sunday morning, according to Sole Links.

The 8 November 2026 post, labelled as an ad, read: "Few sizes via End. NIGO x J-HOPE x Nike Air Force 1 '01", with a link to the retailer's product page.

What the shoe is

End's product page lists the pair as the Nike x Nigo x J-Hope Air Force 1 '01 in Burnt Sunrise, style code IO8766-800. The retailer describes Burnt Sunrise leather with metallic gold overlays and squirrel graphics on the heel and sockliner, a nod to the BTS member's "hobicore" style, and says the design looks back to early-1990s Tokyo.

How Restock Rally labels this

Sole Links posts are affiliate-style availability alerts, not Nike or End inventory statements. "Few sizes" means the account saw limited stock when it posted; sizes can sell through within minutes. Check End's page yourself for current sizes and price before you decide, and be wary of resale listings well above retail.
