Sneaker raffles and draws

We list a raffle only after we confirm it on the retailer's own page. There are no verified open raffles on this page right now.

How raffles work

A raffle or draw lets you enter for the chance to buy a pair at retail. Retailers pick winners at random and charge only the winners. Nike runs many launches on the SNKRS app as timed draws. Enter only through the retailer's own site or app, never through a link that asks for a fee to enter.

Staying safe

  • Check the web address belongs to the retailer.
  • No genuine retailer raffle charges an entry fee.
  • Watch for winner emails that ask you to pay through a new link; go to the retailer directly instead.
